Sermon Title: First things

Passage: Genesis 1:1-5, 28-31, 2:1-3

Big Idea: The triune God is our Creator, the One whom all creatures should worship and obey

In the beginning, God

God created everything

By his word, God has created and sustains the world

Creatures respond with worship and obedience

Worship only one Creator

Study the ordered Creation

Obey the Creator God

Time: T= 7 (T=6) Thursday 10:10pm

Creation by word: And God said. The divine creation is effortless, voluntary and rational. God commands it and it is so. Hebrew text not as clear that it’s creation ex nihilo but it affirms the work of one God and defends divine sovereignty against Hellenistic insistence on the eternity of matter.

Creation is presented as a differentiated totality: its very diversity is part of the process that God declares to be good. The creature is good by virtue of its standing with its creator. “The world is not preserved for its own sake but for the sake of the sight of God” Belief in this divinely ordained order in creation pervades the OT.
