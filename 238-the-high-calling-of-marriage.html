The High calling of Marriage

Passage: Revelations 21:1-4

Marriage is given by God for the well-being of mankind, to help them live out the gospel of grace, and is patterned upon the union between Christ and his Church

1. God gives the high calling of marriage

What’s the secret to having a long marriage?

To answer that, a good place to start might be to ask the couple who had the longest marriage.

According to the Guinness Book of Record Herbert Fisher and Zelmyra Fisher had the longest marriage recorded.

They were married for 86 years and 290 days.

Hebrbet and Zelmyra grew up to be best friends in North Carolina and they married when Herbert was 18 and Zelmyra was 16.

What was their secret?

They posted this on Twitter (now known as X) back in 2010: “A friend is for life; our marriage has lasted a lifetime.”

That’s beautiful, isn’t it?

Perhaps the couple with the shortest marriage can learn from them.

Apparently, a couple in Kuwait got married at the courthouse and the wife tripped over and fell.

Rather than helping the bride, the groom called her “stupid” and so she marched right back in to ask for a divorce from the judge who just married them.

How long did their marriage last? Three minutes or 180 seconds.

That’s crazy!

Now, I’m thinking for most of us who are married here, our marriage is somewhere between three minutes and 86 years.

While many of us would be shocked and saddened by those who lasted a short time, we would be pleasantly surprised and would congratulate those who have lasted decades.

That’s because we know that marriage is a high calling, and it takes a lot of work.

Even if we put in the work, it might not always turn out the way we wanted it to.

For the Christian, the calling is even higher because the Bible teaches us to live out a Christian marriage.

Our world might tell us that if you don’t like the person anymore, you divorce them for amicable reasons.

But the Bible tells us that marriage is a life-long union and you don’t simply divorce them because you don’t like the person anymore.

We’ll be spending more time on the topic of divorce in the coming weeks.

But one thing is clear: The bible teaches that marriages are given by God to last a life-time.

In marriages, we promise each other that it’s “till death do us part”.

It’s a high calling to pursue and come into a Christian marriage.

Why would anyone want to enter into a Christian marriage?

Why is the high calling of Christian marriage worth pursuing?

That’s what we’ll be looking at today.

We’ll be looking at a number of passages, so make sure you have your Bible ready.

Why is the high calling of Christian marriage worth pursuing?

Before we look into it, let’s pray,

“Our heavenly Father, thank you for giving us the wonderful institution of marriage. By your Spirit, please help us now to understand and to live by what you command about marriage. By your Son, let us come with confidence before you, knowing that our sins have been forgiven because of what he’s done, and in whose name we pray, Amen.”

One of the best way for us to get a grasp of what the Bible says about a topic is to use what I call the Seven Stars method ( 指向耶稣的7颗星星).

These are seven sets of doctrines or teachings that help us see how Christians are to understand a particular subject in light of Jesus.

Let’s see if you remember it:

Firstly,

Creation: God is the Creator, we are the creatures

Try drawing them in your booklet as we go along.

Fall: God is the Holy One, we are the sinners.

(Do you remember them now?)

Judgement: God is the Judge, we are the judged.

Salvation: God is Saviour, in Christ we are saved.

Resurrection: Christ is risen, those in Christ will be raised in him.

Regeneration: God sent His Spirit; his Spirit lives in us.

The Last Things: God’s focus on the Last Day is Christ; our hope on the Last Day must also be Christ

Do you remember now as you draw them down?

These seven stars are what we’re going to use as we look at the Topic of marriage.

Let’s start with the first one.

God is the Creator; we are the creatures.

God created everything we know, including marriage.

Marriage is not a social construct; it’s not something that we made up.

Marriage is God-given.

And yet, today, people have all kinds of strange ideas about marriage.

In most cultures around the world since the beginning, humans have rightly understood marriage as the lifelong union between a man and a woman.

But today, people make marriage into whatever they want it to be.

Some people marry themselves to bridges, others to towers, and still others to anime characters.

It seems very strange to marry yourself to a non-human.

But why would it be strange if marriage is just a social construct?

If it’s a social construct, people can make up what marriage is according to whatever they wish.

However, though marriages can look very different in different communities, marriage is not a social construct.

You can try to change the definition to suit your case, but that doesn’t mean you can make marriage into something it’s not.

Marriage is a God given institute.

You can call yourself the King of the world and sincerely believe it, but that doesn’t make it true.

Marriage is what God has given for all people, all races, across all times.

God is the Creator of marriage and he’s recorded it as one of the earliest institutions that he’s made all the way back in Genesis 1 and 2.

We see this in Genesis 2:21-24,

“ 21 So the Lord God caused a deep sleep to come over the man, and he slept. God took one of his ribs and closed the flesh at that place. 22 Then the Lord God made the rib he had taken from the man into a woman and brought her to the man. 23 And the man said:

This one, at last, is bone of my bone and flesh of my flesh; this one will be called “woman,” for she was taken from man.

24 This is why a man leaves his father and mother and bonds with his wife, and they become one flesh.”

It’s God who’ve given the woman to the man.

God presents the woman to the man, like a father giving away his daughter to the groom.

It’s beautiful!

Jesus himself refers to this in Matthew 19:5-6

“ and he also said, ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two (male and female) will become one flesh’? 6 So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore, what God has joined together, let no one separate.”

That’s what God gave us.

Marriage is God-given and it’s non-negotiable.

According to the Bible, going into a marriage is a high calling, it honourable and it takes hard work.

Firstly, going into a marriage takes commitment.

The Bible says that a man is to leave father and mother, in order to begin a new family unit with his wife.

The bond he has with his wife is even stronger than the bond between him and his parents.

It’s a lifelong commitment to be utterly devoted to one person of the opposite gender.

In a Christian wedding ceremony, the groom and the bride vow to be there for one another, for better for worse, for richer for poorer, in sickness and in health.

It’s a high calling to be married because you are called to be there for the person, always.

It takes great commitment but there are also great benefits as well.

God has created marriages so that children can be born in families where they see the commitment and the faithfulness of their parents at work.

It’s a blessing for the couple to have children in marriage;

it’s a blessing for the children to grow up in a family where their father and mother show commitment and faithfulness in their marriage.

It’s a blessing for children to be in a loving home.

Children, if you are in a loving home, please thank God in your prayers for your parents.

Being married doesn’t automatically lead to a loving home; your parents have worked hard to make it happen!

It’s a huge blessing!

Now, not every couple will have children and different couples will respond to this differently.

Some will enjoy the extra time that they have as couples; others will yearn for many years to have children.

Those couples with children must be mindful of those couples who might be yearning for children; and the working women with no children must never look down on those mothers whose full-time work is to look after the children.

While not every couple can have children, all married couples have the blessing of sharing exclusive intimacy with one another.

It’s so good to share sexual intimacy within the institution of a Christian marriage!

You have someone who belongs exclusively to you when it comes to sexual intimacy, and you also belong exclusively to your spouse.

This is something exclusive to your marriage; something you share with no one else.

As bad as our society may have fallen, most of our society still sees this as good and vital.

To cheat on your wife or your husband is bad, because we recognise that having sexual intimacy between a husband and wife is good.

It’s a delight to be faithful to one person in a lifelong relationship.

In marriage, we have a delightful faithfulness!

We know that having life-long marriages are good, and that having a faithful relationship in a marriage is good.

But why is it still so hard?

Because God is the Holy One and we are sinners.

As sinners, we are tempted to not devote our lives to one person.

We are tempted to be unfaithful.

Our society no longer see exclusive intimacy in a marriage as a non-negotiable.

Instead, they would rather live in a de-facto relationship, or not in any long term relationships, because they don’t want to commit themselves to one person in a life-long relationship.

And even though it’s good to have children, many couples refuse to have children, because having a family will mean additional commitments.

Many young couples in Australia, and I heard in China also, are deliberately choosing not to have children because they consider having a family too expensive.

Instead of seeing children as a blessing, they see it as a curse.

Sadly, many children suffer terrible fates as result.

Instead of following the instructions of the our Creator on what to do with our marriages, our society made up our own rules about marriage.

As a result, we see chaos in our society.

We see sinners go from bad to worse and we tragedies upon tragedies.

As we continue to go down our own path of rebellion against God’s way, God’s judgement falls on our society.

We become a society whose hearts have grown cold to God and to fellow human beings.

There’s a high cost to our society when we rebel against what God has said about marriage.

There’s a sense in which God doesn’t even need to do anything additional to bring judgement on us.

When we rebel against God, we naturally bring judgement on ourselves.

Experts tell us that, generally speaking, there’s a better health outcome and stronger economic stability in stable marriages.

In contrast, there’s a high cost to divorce and childbearing.

It’s estimated that each year the cost of divorce in Australia is $40 billion.

That’s just the cost of the divorce.

On top of that, there are emotional components such as higher rates of crime, depression and long-term poverty that comes with divorce.

There’s a very high emotional cost as well.

I have close family members and close friends who have gone through divorces.

Some were young couples, others are older couples.

It was very hard to see them go through divorces.

The effects of these divorces had a massive impact on their friends and families for many years.

Some of these are very good Christians who do trust in Jesus.

They have worked hard, very hard, to make their marriages work.

But for one reason or another, it didn’t turn out how they wanted, and in many instances, there were factors beyond their control.

It’s hard work!

It’s hard work because to be married is a high calling, a very high calling.

Who wants to get married?

As hard as it is for the non-Christian, the calling for a Christian marriage is even higher!

The husband is called to be sacrificial, willing to die for his wife, as Jesus would.

And the wife is called to be submissive to their husband, as the Church submits to Christ.

There’s a sense in which being sacrificial and submissive applies to every marriage but in the Bible, it applies explicitly to Christians.

Christians, when you made the vow to marry your spouse on your wedding day, that’s what you signed up for!

It’s hard work! It’s High calling!

Why would anyone want to be married as Christians?

To find out, you need to come back next week!

I’ve prepared far more for this sermon than I intended, and so I’ll continue with this next week.

But I don’t want you to go away thinking that we shouldn’t get married at all!

And so, please come with me to Revelation 21:1-2,

“Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth; for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea was no more. 2 I also saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared like a bride adorned for her husband.”

This is the description of the Last Day, when Jesus comes.

On that day, the old will pass away and Jesus will replace it with the new.

The way the Bible describes it is like a wedding, with the new Jerusalem, God’s Holy City, where Jesus’ followers will be, coming down like a bride for her husband.

In this case, the husband will be God himself.

On that day, the followers of Jesus will live with God the Father, God the Son and God the Spirit, forever and ever.

That’s what all marriages are ultimately pointing towards.

God has patterned each marriage upon the union between Christ and His Church.

Yes, Christian marriage is a high calling, but it’s a high calling worth pursuing for, because in our marriages, we somehow point to the true happily ever after between Christ and His Church.

To find out more, please come back next week!

According to God’s word, the Bible, why is marriage such a high calling?

Why is it good to be married according to God’s word? Why is it not good to make our own rule?

Friends, pursuing the high calling of marriage is a high calling from God.

And so, honour the high calling of Christian Marriage.

Honour Christian marriage in your own home.

If you are married, honour your spouse.

If you are unmarried and living at home with a Christian couple, that is, all you sons and daughters, congratulate them.

Don’t wait until their 86th anniversary.

Congratulate them now!

If you are unmarried but know a Christian couple, and I think that’s most of us, pray for the Christian couples that you know, because it’s hard.

Pray also for the couples with one spouse who’s Christian and one who’s not, because it’s hard, very hard, especially for the Christian spouse.

Friends, honour the high calling of Christian Marriage.Let’s pray,

Please make us into a faithful people, faithful to you, faithful to our spouse, faithful to your people. We know we cannot follow Jesus on our own, we know that we cannot pursue the high calling of Christian marriage on our own. And so, please by your Spirit, transform us into the people you called us to be. For we pray in Jesus’ name, Amen.”
