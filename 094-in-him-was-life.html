In Him Was Life

John 1:3-4 · Advent 2022
Big IdeaAll things were created through Jesus, whose life is the light of man.

In him was life

Passage: John 1.3-4

Big Idea: All things were created through Jesus, whose life is the light of man.

Life is precious!

Life comes from the Living Word

Life is precious!

No matter where you’re from, it’s almost universally agreed that life is precious.

We celebrate the birth of a new baby because her life brings us joy.

It’s a good thing to be alive!

And we keep celebrating the birth of someone by celebrating their birthday.

Hands up anyone who’s been to a birthday party lately?

We celebrate their birthday because it’s great to see them alive after all these years!

You’re born? That’s great! You’ve been living for 80 years? Even better!

And that’s why, when someone is saved from the jaws of death, when they’re rescued from danger, we celebrate!

Why, because their life was in danger, they were in danger of losing it.

But they are now they’re safe from harm; they can keep on living.

It’s a good thing to keep living and it’ll be a bad thing to lose a life.

But why do we see life as precious?

Could it be because we enjoy life, that living gives us happiness, and that’s why we treasure it?

That’s why we find life precious?

I hope you do have a happy life, but anyone who’s lived for a little while will know that life is more than just a series of happy moments.

Throughout life we face hard times.

Our life is often filled with as many sad moments as the happy moments.

Sometimes, we have more of one than the other.

But the happy and the sad moments, the easy and the difficult moments – they are all part of what we call “life”.

Being alive is more than just about being happy.

There are many moments in our lives when we are not happy but we’re still living.

We don’t stop living because we’re not happy.

Just because we face sad moments in life doesn’t make life any less precious.

In fact, to say that life is only precious if we’re happy is to insult millions upon millions of people around the world who live unhappy lives not due to their own choice.

Are their lives less precious than those who lead happier lives?

Absolutely not!

Life had to be more than just about being happy.

Is it possible for life to be precious in and of itself?

Is there something inherently precious in this thing we call life?

Yes, life is inherently precious; it’s been so not only for the tens of thousands of years that life has appeared here on Planet Earth.

Today’s Bible passage tells us why life is precious by taking us back to the beginning, back to the creation of life here on earth.

In fact, the passage goes even further and takes us to the very heart of God, before the creation.

Life comes from the Living Word

Look with me at today’s passage, John 1:3-4, “All things were created through him, and apart from him not one thing was created that has been created. 4 In him was life, and that life was the light of men.”

The Gospel of John begins by referring to the Word, who was with God, who was God, who was with God in the beginning.

As we read on, we know that the Word is Jesus.

Jesus, the Word was with God in the very beginning.

All things were created through him; without him nothing was created.

Everything that has been created owe their existence to Jesus; everything!

All the stars in space owe their existence to Jesus.

All the mountains and all the oceans owe their existence to Jesus.

Everyone here at Church, and everyone who has ever lived, owe their existence to Jesus.

It’s an absolute statement.

And to make sure that we understood, the second part of verse three says nothing is created without him.

Through Jesus, everything that is created is created.

Without Jesus, nothing is created that has been created.

Everything that has ever existed owe their very exitence to God who created them all through Jesus.

God created nothing without Jesus.

God created everything through Jesus.

Imagine a blank canvas; there’s nothing on it.

After months of painting, perhaps months of research before that, the painter starts painting.

Everything we see on the final product is a result of the painter putting his brush to work.

The painting didn’t paint itself; it was painted by the painter.

Without the painter, without the brush, there would be no painting.

In the Bible, we see God the Father as the Creator.

God the Father created everything through Jesus.

Whether something is living and non-living, God created them all through Jesus, God created all things.

When God created the heavens and the earth, he spoke and they came into being.

God said, “Let there be light, and there was light.”

And so, when the Bible describes Jesus as the Word, it’s describing Jesus as the One through Whom God made the world.

It’s through Jesus, the Word, that God made the world.

Through Jesus, God brings something that he has had for all eternity to the world.

Through Jesus, God brings life to the world.

Look with me again at verse 4, “4 In him was life, and that life was the light of men.”

Life was in God the Father and in God the Son before the beginning of the world.

There was never a time when God wasn’t alive.

Jesus is alive but not because life has come to him.

Life is there, life is alive, as it were, because of him.

Jesus is not under this phenomenon we call life.

Rather it’s the other way around.

Life is part of him; in Him was life

Jesus owns life; Life belongs to him.

It’s through Jesus, the Word of God, that God creates living things and non-living things.

Atheists believe that life comes from lifeless things.

They argue that there’s no living God and so life must have come from lifeless things.

They believe that there was a time when the lifeless came to life.

They haven’t seen it; they haven’t been able to prove it, but they believe it.

What the Bible teaches is that it’s the other way around.

The Life-giving God has always been there and it’s through Jesus the Word, God the Son, that he made everything.

It’s through him, in whom is life, that all that’s living and all that’s lifeless are created.

This thing we call life, is not an accident, but a gift from the living God who has been living for ever and ever.

That’s why life is precious.

Life, the life that is in Jesus, is the light of men.

The life of Jesus is what gives us that spark as men and women, boys and girls.

This life that we have is a reflection, a poor reflection but a reflection nevertheless, of something that God has had before the beginning of time, before the beginning of the world.

Jesus had this life in him forever and it will be with Jesus forever.

In Him was life.

And through him, God has given each living thing, each living person, life.

And so, Christians don’t see our life as our own.

Christians see life as a precious gift from God.

As Christians, we realise that don’t own life.

The life that we have, the life that each one of us have, whether we’re Christians or not, are from Jesus.

That’s why each life is precious.

That’ why it’s Christians who have started hospitals in so many countries around the world.

Many hospitals in China, for example, has a red cross on them, because it’s Christians who have started them.

As Christians, we value the life of everyone, whether they’re rich or poor, because life is a gift of Jesus.

Closer to home, our work with Anglicare to have the Mobile Pantry because we want to support those who are undergoing difficulties.

We want to look after each person because each life, each soul, is a precious gift from God, given through Jesus.

That’s why we celebrate the birth of Jesus.

The birth of a baby is precious.

But the birth of the life giver himself is the most precious of all.

At Christmas, we celebrate the One who created world.

We celebrate the One who gave everything that exist their existence.

We celebrate the One who gave everything that is alive life.

Jesus has come into our world!

Christmas is the world’s biggest birthday party.

It celebrates the birth of Jesus, through whom all things were created, in whom is life.

As we celebrate this Birthday party, and as we celebrate all birthday parties,

May we remember the precious gift that Jesus has given us.

May we celebrate life, this wonderful, precious gift from the everlasting God.

May we remember and celebrate Jesus.

Amen.
