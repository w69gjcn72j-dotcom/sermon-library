Learning to walk and talk in the School of Christ

Passage: Ephesians 4:17-32

Since you are in the School of Christ, put off the old self, be renewed, and put on the new self, speaking and living the truth of God’s forgiveness for you in Christ.

Take your off your old rotten person

Be renewed and put on the new God-like person

Speak and live as a person forgiven by God

Good morning, everyone and welcome to St Paul’s Anglican Church in Kogarah.

My name is David Yung, the minister of this Church and it’s great to see you here!

As some of you know, I’ll be going on long service leave with my family tomorrow.

We’ll be having a short holiday with the extended family, and then my family and I will fly to Hong Kong.

I still remember going back to Hong Kong for the first time with my family, back when Zoe was 6, Ezekiel was 5 and Caitlyn was 2.

As children who grew up in Australia, both Jenny and I, along with our children, can often feel like we stand out among the crowd, even when we don’t want to.

But not in Hong Kong! In Hong Kong, we blend right in.

We look the same as most of the other people in Hong Kong; we’re just one face in the crowd!

That is until we speak; as soon as we speak, people can tell that we’re not locals.

That’s when we look forward to coming back home here in Australia, where people understand us when we speak, even if we do look different.

While some of us might like to stand out from the crowd, I suspect most of us want to blend in.

We don’t want people to think that we’re strange or weird; we don’t want people to treat us as foreigners.

We want people to like us and people like us when we’re like them, when we’re one of them.

But here, in today’s passage, the Bible tells us not to be like the rest.

The Bible tells us not to blend in: verse 17, “Therefore, I say this and testify in the LORD: You should no longer live as the Gentiles live, in the futility of their thoughts.”

Why shouldn’t Christians blend in? Why shouldn’t Christians be like the rest of the world?

Why won’t Christians want to be like the world around them?

Please open up to our passage in Ephesians chapter 4, beginning from verse 17.

We’ll see three things in today’s passage:

Take your off your old rotten person

Be renewed and put on the new God-like person

Speak and live as a person forgiven by God

Before we look into it, let’s pray:

“Father, by your Holy Spirit, please open the eyes of our hearts that we might see the truth of the gospel, as you’ve shown us in the Lord Jesus, in whose name we pray, Amen.”

Take your off your old rotten person

Our passage begins with a strong warning from the Apostle Paul: “Therefore, I say this and testify in the Lord: You should no longer walk as the Gentiles do, in the futility of their thoughts.”

The Bible tells us not to think like the rest of the world because their thoughts are futile, or useless.

They think, but their thinking is a waste of time.

What does that mean? Isn’t thinking a good thing?

Thinking is a good thing; God created us to be thinkers.

To be able to think is a wonderful gift from God.

We’re taught to think in schools and at work and when we don’t think we get into all sorts of trouble.

When we act without thinking, people will tell us, “What were you thinking? Were you thinking?”

Many people will tell you that the more you think, the better you will be; the more the world thinks, the better the world will be.

However, the problem is not how much we think but whether what we think are good things.

The problem is whether the content of our thinking, our thoughts, are good.

We can think about a lot of things, but they might not be useful.

In fact, they might even be harmful.

For example, the number of bank robberies in the United States is at its lowest in the last 50 years.

Is it because people are nicer and there are less greedy and desperate people committing crimes?

No! It’s because criminals are robbing banks and other institutions online.

They hack into a bank’s system or steal someone’s password, or pretend to be someone they’re not, in order to steal their money.

These are smart, well-educated, cyber criminals.

We now know more as a society than we’ve ever been as the human race, but our society, our world is not getting better.

It’s because we’re still futile in our thoughts.

And our understanding has been darkened; we can’t see reality clearly.

More importantly, we can’t see the reality of God clearly.

There are more people who say that they don’t believe in God, not because they understand more, but because they understand less.

There’s an ignorance in them that refuses to believe in God and so they’re excluded from the life of God.

They’re ignorant of God and so they ignore God, deliberately turning away from him.

Their hearts turn hard against the things of God and intentionally refuse to be grateful to God for what he’s done for them.

As a result, they become callous – not caring about God or people, not loving God or people.

Their focus is on their own happiness, usually at great shame to themselves and great cost to others.

Look with me at verse 19, “They became callous and gave themselves over to promiscuity for the practice of every kind of impurity with a desire for more and more.”

To be promiscuous is to live with moral depravity, not willing to live according to what’s right and have no shame of doing what’s wrong.

And the world cheers their own on as they all fall further into depravity.

The world tells them to be proud, to not be ashamed of what you do, even when it’s morally wrong.

In fact, the more wrong you are, the more depraved you are, the more cheers you’ll get from the world.

That’s what a world without God desires; it wants its people to fall more and more into depravity.

What we believe about God has a direct impact on the way we behave.

If we believe in God, there’s someone we’re accountable to.

If we don’t believe in God, we’re only accountable to ourselves.

But if you’re a Christian, you haven’t come to learn Christ that way.

Look with me at verses 20-21, “But that is not how you came to know Christ, assuming you heard about him and were taught by him, as the truth is in Jesus,”

When you came to know Christ, literally, when you learned Christ, he hasn’t taught that you can live in whatever way that you want.

That’s not the way of Christ.

That’s not what Jesus Christ teaches in the School of Christ, as it were.

In the School of Christ, you learn about significance of who Jesus is and what he taught.

The School of Christ is the most excellent school that you can ever be a student of, because in the School of Christ, you will learn the Truth, for the Truth is in Christ.

In Christ, you will learn the Truth about God, the truth about the world, the truth about yourself.

In the School of Christ, you will learn that world’s thoughts are futile and the life that it encourages is promiscuous.

You will learn what it means to take off your old way of life.

You will leave their empty and rotten thoughts outside.

For you’re now in the School of Christ.

Last week, I heard the story about a Special Religious Education Co-ordinator who met a young man who wanted to teach the Bible in a very rough boys’ school.

The young man hasn’t taught the Bible in a school before and the co-ordinator thought it would be too difficult for this young man.

But the young man replied, “Na, the boys won’t be a problem.”

Why? Because this young man was once a rebel himself.

He got kicked out of many schools and no school wanted him.

Our education system wasn’t helping him.

However, in time he became a Christian and wanted to teach the Bible in schools.

For him the hardest part is not the rough students; that’s the easy part.

The hard part for him was how to teach the Bible.

This young man is now a follower of Jesus Christ, in the School of Christ, teaching other young men about Christ in their school.

The thinking of the world is rotten and futile. Why does anyone want to go back into it?

And so the Bible tells us to take off the old and rotten person inside us, to not be fooled by the deceitful desires that belonged to the old and rotten person.

Be renewed and put on the new God-like person

Instead, we’re to be renewed in the spirits of our minds.

Look with me at verses 23-24, “to be renewed in the spirit of your minds, 24 and to put on the new self, the one created according to God’s likeness in righteousness and purity of the truth.”

You can’t just stop being someone who think futile thoughts.

A radical change inside us is necessary.

We need a renewal of the spirit of your mind.

Two weeks ago, as I was preparing for the service on Sunday, the computer suddenly decided to stop working.

We tried many ways to fix it and somehow, by God’s grace, we got it working for two hours, while we had the Sunday service.

Almost as soon as the service was over, the computer went down again.

As a result, we had to get a new computer straight away.

Our old PC was taken down and we replaced it with a new PC.

For those of us who genuinely trust in Jesus, God created a new self, a new person, inside of us.

In Jesus, we were created in God’s likeness; we were created to be like God in righteousness and in purity of truth.

While God created everyone in his image, we have rebelled against God and brought sin and corruption to his perfect world.

However, God has not abandoned us but sent Jesus Christ to suffer the consequences of our actions.

Jesus died for our promiscuous actions and acts of rebellion, even though he was perfect in every way.

But Jesus rose from the dead on the third day to show that he has power over death.

When he was back by his Father’s side, He sent His Spirit, the Holy Spirit, to make us into a new creation.

By His Holy Spirit, God’s own Holy Spirit, Jesus made us into a new person, one that’s renewed in the spirit of our minds, day by day.

As someone transformed from within, we will speak and act according to God’s purity and righteousness.

To be pure means not desiring promiscuity or to think futile thoughts.

Instead, a person who is pure will follow Jesus

They will learn what it means to live by the death and the resurrection of the Lord Jesus.

They will live according to the righteousness of Jesus for Jesus has made them righteous.

They will treasure the Church for in the church, they see other members who are also made new by Jesus.

They will go with the other followers of Christ to learn Christ in the School of Christ.

The Anglican theologian John Stott has put it beautifully when he said, 'If heathen degradation is due to the futility of their minds, then Christian righteousness depends on the constant renewing of our minds'. (Stott)

Our minds, particularly what we think of God in our minds, will determine how we live.

A non-believer’s mind will think futile thoughts which will lead them down to moral degradation.

A believer’s mind will be renewed daily so that he / she can live out the righteousness and holiness of God as someone saved by Jesus.

There are many good things about the life that the Bible teaches.

Most people would recognise that righteousness is a good thing and many will respect either holiness or some sort of spirituality.

Even in the every-day language used by non-Christians, we often see a Christian connection, like #Blessed / #感恩.

These are nice things to say but words spoken and deeds done aren’t helpful if there are no changes on the inside.

We can’t stop the rottenness of the world without changing the inside of individual persons.

We need to be created anew on the inside before putting on the new person outside.

We need to repent and be forgiven by God in Christ first before living the life of a Christian.

3. Speak and live as a person forgiven by God

Once we’ve been renewed, we’ll speak and live as person who has been forgiven by God.

There’s a saying in English that goes, you talk to the talk, but do you walk the walk?

It describes someone who might say the right thing, but do their lives show that they do what they say?

Does someone’s walk match the way he talks?

If you have been renewed as a Christian, you will need to make sure that you walk the walk, just as you have talked the talk.

More than that, you have to make sure that your talks are actually the right kind of talk.

In verses 25-32, Paul lists four ways that Christians relate to one another, two of those ways will be about the talk, and the other two will focus on the walk.

While what Paul says applies to the people outside of Church as well, the focus is on how Christians relate to people in their Church.

Firstly, put away lying and speak the truth, each one to his neighbour.

Be careful what you say to other members of the Church.

Don’t spread lies that goes against the gospel.

Think about whether what you’re saying is in line with the gospel before you say it.

Speak the truth of the gospel to one another, for we are members of one another.

Second, in verse 26, “Be angry and do not sin. Don’t let the sun go down on your anger, and don’t give the devil an opportunity. “

Be angry and do not sin, set a time limit on your anger to last only until sundown.

Be angry? Yes, there are times when it’s right to be angry.

We ought to be angry at sin, and the rottenness of the world.

However, because we’re sinful people we tend to sin when we’re angry.

And so, we should set a limit on our anger to last only until the sun goes down

Don’t let your anger simmer.

Don’t let anger rot your soul, even if you have a good reason to be angry.

For if you let anger simmer within you, you give the devil a foothold, a place to stay in your heart.

Don’t give the devil even one inch of your heart.

Don’t forget that it’s a spiritual battle.

In the context of the Church, don’t stay angry at others in the church.

Not only will it rot you; it will also rot the Church.

Third, don’t steal.

There are lots of material things that we want but we don’t want to work for it.

And so there will be moments when we’re tempted to steal to get to those things quickly.

It might be embezzling money from work or from home, it might be wage theft, where you steal from your employees by not paying them appropriately.

Or even more commonly, it might be stealing by not working the hours you were paid to work for.

That’s what a person with futile thoughts would do.

But if your mind is renewed, you will work hard, you will labour with your own hands.

You’ll do it not for yourselves but to provide for others in need.

Rather than going by the motto: Work hard / play hard,

You will work hard / give hard.

Finally, if your mind is renewed and you’ve put on the new person, there’s to be no foul language from your mouths.

Paul comes back to the topic of speech.

Foul language includes things like, bad language, swearing, gossiping, slandering, as well as saying destructive and thoughtless words to bring people down.

You’ve probably have met people like that.

Every time you see them, they only have bad things to say about you or other people and never anything good.

That’s foul language and should not be found in the mouths of God’s people.

Instead, we’re to say things that build people up.

The Bible is not talking about flattery, but words that give grace, in accordance with the gospel.

If we use foul language instead of speaking words that build people up, we will grieve the Holy Spirit.

People who call themselves Christians who speak foul language to other Christians grieve the Holy Spirit,

For they tear people down instead of bringing them up in the love of Christ.

The Devil wants you to be angry at one another and to speak foul words to one another.

And the Holy Spirit is grieved when we use foul language on one another.

You attack God’s Holy Spirit, you give him grief, when you attack fellow Christians with foul language.

If you’re a Christian, God has sealed you with His Holy Spirit for the day of redemption.

You belong to holy God; Don’t speak words unbecoming of a Christian.

If you belong to a holy God, you will let go of all bitterness, anger and wrath (all the things that rot you on the inside),

As well as shouting and slander (all the words that tear down the unity of God’s people).

You will let malice go as well.

Instead you will do this: Look with me at verse 32, “And be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving one another, just as God also forgave you in Christ.”

Instead of being angry, instead of using foul language when you’re wronged, you will forgive.

Instead of lying and shouting and slandering, you will speak the truth of the gospel, showing the kindness in your speech.

Instead of stealing from others, you will give to others, because you’re compassionate and give to those in need.

That’s how Christians are to relate to one another: we relate to one another according to how God has treated us in Christ.

As God has forgiven us in Christ, we are to forgive one another.

We’re to live according to the forgiveness of God in Christ.

Why won’t Christians want to be like the world around them?

They recognise that the thinking of the world is futile, since it does not have the truth of Jesus.

Christians used to be like them, but because they’re renewed, they’ve put off the old person, and put on their new person, created according to God’s likeness.

We will say no to futile thoughts and yes to a mind renewed.

It’s only when we’re renewed that we can put on new self, speaking and acting in light of God’s forgiveness of us in Jesus.

And so, Say No to the old self, be renewed, and say Yes to the new self

Over the past few weeks, I’ve attended a few farewell talks at school where students said that they were like shy and scared when they came to school but now they are confident and equipped as they finish school.

Friends, if you’re a Christian, then you’ve entered into the School of Christ.

Before you entered you use to walk and talk like the world, but now you walk and talk as someone forgiven,

someone who has hope, someone who walks not according to a rotten and dying world but according Jesus Christ, the ever-living, eternal Son of God

When you talk with others at church, will you sound like the world, or will you echo what Jesus has said and taught?

When others see you work, will they see someone who steals, or someone who labours to provide for the needs of others?

Friends, Say No to the old self, be renewed, and say Yes to the new self

Here are the three questions for this week:

When do I speak words of truth (that is the gospel) to other people at Church?

Where in my life am I giving the devil a foothold?

Do my words to other Christians build them up, or do I grieve the Holy Spirit by my foul, thoughtless words?”

Friends, if you’re a Christian, you’re in the School of Christ.

In the School of Christ, learn to say No to the old self, be renewed, and say Yes to the new self.

Let’s pray, “Father, thank you for saving us in Christ and showing us the gospel of your salvation. Please forgive us when we use words to destroy, to bring down, rather than to build up. Please forgive us for thinking more like the world that’s rotting away instead of lifting our thoughts to Jesus. By your Spirit, help us to talk and walk according to the gospel of forgiveness, as shown in your Son. Let us not grieve your Holy Spirit by our thoughtless words. May the thoughts of our hearts and the words of our mouths be pleasing in your sight. For Jesus’ sake, Amen”
