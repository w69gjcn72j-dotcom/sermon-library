Remove the Old Leaven and Remain Holy

1 Corinthians 5:1-13 · 2021
Big IdeaChrist our Passover lamb has been sacrificed, so clean out the old leaven and celebrate with sincerity and truth.

Remove the old leaven and Remain Holy

1 Corinthians 5:1-8: Sin is like old leaven

1 Corinthians 5:9-13: Remain holy

Let’s bow our heads together in prayer:

Loving Heavenly Father, please guard me as I preach the sermon

Let the one who speaks and the one who hears benefit

And that our lives be built up

We pray in the name of Jesus Christ, Amen

Over the past four weeks, Rev David and Pastor Roger took us through the first four chapters of 1 Corinthians.

I don’t know what your impression of the Corinthian Church is.

Anyway, the impression that I had is really not good.

The church in Corinth is full of jealousy and strife.

There are those who cause divisions and form groups.

There are even some who look down on the preacher

Today, we will see even worse things happen in the church

It’s so bad that it doesn’t happen even among the Gentiles

If you have the Bible in your hand, please turn with me to 1 Corinthians chapter 5 verses 1 and 2:

“It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and the kind of sexual immorality that is not even tolerated[a] among the Gentiles—a man is sleeping with his father’s wife. 2 And you are arrogant! Shouldn’t you be filled with grief and remove from your congregation the one who did this?”

The translation of "accepting his stepmother" in the Chinese Bible is quite euphemistic.

The meaning in English and the original text is this:

the man and his father’s wife had a relationship that shouldn’t have happened.

And they continue to live together like a couple.

Paul sadly sighed that such adulterous relationships don’t even happen among Gentiles,

And yet, it happened in the very church in Corinth that he had founded

What makes Paul even more distressed is the attitude within the church on this matter

Not only were they not sorrowful; they were outright arrogant,

What an insult to God!

Corinth was a commercial city in Greece,

It was very prosperous under Rome rule.

But while it was materially abundant, it was also full of all kinds of sins.

Because its culture was deeply influenced by the Greeks,

Many Corinthians believe that the human flesh and the human soul are completely independent.

They believe that the sin of the flesh does not affect the holiness of the soul

And so the people lived very promiscuous lives.

Although the gospel had already been preached at Corinth

And although Paul had already established the Corinthian Church

The believers in the Corinthian Church had yet to completely reject this wrong way of thinking.

Instead, they merged this way of thinking into the gospel of Christ; they brought a mistaken teaching into the church.

They taught this very wrong view:

They said, “A person who has accepted the gospel continues to sin in the flesh,

It will not affect the soul's salvation in the future.”

And so, those who have been doing such horrible sins, along with the congregation in the Church,

are arrogant and proud even though they should’ve been grieving instead.

Those who think that Christians such as themselves have a special authority to commit such sins, think that this is great!

However, it’s in light of this error that Paul makes a serious accusation against the Corinthian church,

Although Paul is not in Corinth,

his heart has always been concerned for the Corinthian church

After hearing about what’s happened among them,

He said that he’s present with them in spirit.

Ask them to drive out the man who sinned.

It is written in Section 4-5:

4 When you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus, and I am with you in spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus, 5 hand that one over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord.”

Paul asked the Corinthian church,

under the authority of Jesus and in the name of Jesus,

to hand the man who did this evil to Satan.

The word "delivered to Satan" here has the meaning of casting the man out the church

He is to be expelled from the church, his name is to be removed from the church.

The Church is telling that person to leave and to enter into the wickedness of Satan,

Under the power of Satan.

He is to take responsibility for his own actions,

He will taste the evil fruits of the plant that he’s sown.

The purpose of this is the hope that his flesh will be destroyed in the process of being handed over to Satan.

It’s the hope of seeing true sorrow, that the man may sincerely repent, so that he will be saved.

For someone who has accepted the gospel and has actively participated in the church

For those who have tasted the Lord’s grace

To be put under the power of Satan,

In contrast to his former life in the Lord’s grace

Would feel horrible.

The hope is that they would feel remorse and repent.

On the day the Lord Jesus returns, before the judgement seat of God, Paul wants see people like these saved.

At the same time, Paul would never allow such sins to remain in the church

knowing that even non-believers in Corinth at that time would’ve thought such what the man had done was shameful.

Instead, the Corinthian church boasted.

And so Paul rebuked the church by saying in verse 6:

“Your boasting is not good. Don’t you know that a little leaven leavens the whole batch of dough?”

I don’t know if you have any experience in using yeast to ferment dough

If yes, please send the answer to us via WeChat, SMS, ZOOM or WhatsApp

Tell us how your fermented dough changes before and after the fermentation process.

Everyone, we have 20 seconds.

I have no experience in making dough with yeast,

But I have eaten it, and I have seen my sister make it

She used lid of the mineral water bottle to measure half a lid full of yeast.

With this small amount she can make a large piece of dough

The yeast itself is active; once it enters the dough

It will rapidly expand, penetrating every part.

The leaven in Paul’s time was made from the dough left over from the last batch.

Even if there is only a small amount, it’s enough to make a new batch.

This is what sin’s corrupting nature is like: from a small amount it can expand into something huge.

Paul compares the Corinthian church to the dough and sin to leaven

Just as surely as active yeast will penetrate the entire dough

Active sin will surely spread to the entire church.

Verses 7-8

"Clean out the old leaven so that you may be a new unleavened batch, as indeed you are. For Christ our Passover lamb has been sacrificed. 8 Therefore, let us observe the feast, not with old leaven or with the leaven of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth."

Paul compares the Corinthians who have believed in the Lord to a new unleavened batch, that is, as a new creation in Christ.

Since Jesus Christ has died for us

we should no longer keep practising the sinful habits and the sinful thoughts,

that we had before believing in the Lord.

This is what it means to remove the old leaven.

This parable does not originate from Paul, but from God

We’ve seen this mentioned as early as the second book of the Old Testament of the Bible

In the Book of Exodus, chapter 12.

At that time, the Israelites celebrated the first Passover according to God’s command.

At the Passover, the sacrifice of the lamb is a sign of the redemption for the people’s sins.

On that night, the Israelites were rescued and fled Egypt.

Since there was a great urgency to leave,

they took the dough that hasn’t been leavened yet and left.

That’s how the Jewish Festival of Unleavened Bread got its name.

At that time, the land in Egypt was full of idols, and the Egyptian Pharaoh did not worship the one true God.

In this way, Egypt was regarded as sinful.

The exodus of the Israelites from Egypt symbolised that they’ve left the sinful world behind and that they were redeemed.

And so, the yeast then symbolises sin

They left Egypt with unleavened dough,

Which symbolises that they cannot enter into their new life with the sin they’ve left behind.

And it all started when the lamb was slaughtered at Passover

This lamb is like a prototype of Jesus Christ

The lamb is slaughtered,

It foreshadows the death of Jesus Christ on the cross for our sins

Paul is using this act in history as a parable.

He tells the Corinthians that because Jesus Christ has died for us and paid for the wages of our sins

We should no longer let any sin live in our lives anymore

We should leave all our sinful habits and sinful thoughts behind us.

We should earnestly strive for holy living.

Verses 9-10

I wrote to you in a letter not to associate with sexually immoral people. 10 I did not mean the immoral people of this world or the greedy and swindlers or idolaters; otherwise you would have to leave the world.”

From here we can see that 1 Corinthians is not the first letter that Paul wrote to the church in Corinth.

At least one earlier letter had been written.

Paul mentioned in that letter not to associate with sexually immoral people.

The Corinthians didn’t understand how to live in their community if they didn’t associate with these sinners,

because at that time Corinth was a city famous for its sexual immorality

From something as small as the design of a wine glass, to something as serious as worshipping a false god in a temple

Sinful behaviour filled the entire city.

Wherever Christians went, they would be likely to encounter people who are sexually immoral, greedy, swindlers, or idol worshippers.

The only way to avoid them is to leave the world.

And so Paul clarifies on this point:

He’s not asking them to not associate with these sinners who are outside the church

However, they’re to forbid such things from happening inside the church

If it happens, it needs to be dealt with immediately

Verses 11-13:

“But actually, I wrote you not to associate with anyone who claims to be a brother or sister and is sexually immoral or greedy, an idolater or verbally abusive, a drunkard or a swindler. Do not even eat with such a person. 12 For what business is it of mine to judge outsiders? Don’t you judge those who are inside? 13 God judges outsiders. Remove the evil person from among you.

If someone in the church claims to be a Christian and yet continues to commit adultery,

Or continues to be greedy, verbally abusive, worship idols, slanders others, or given to drunkenness, and don’t repentance

Then we must drive such people out of the church

We mustn’t associate with such people anymore

We are not to even eat with them.

The purpose of this is to keep the church holy

Paul emphasized that no matter how small the sin

It will expand like yeast

It must be removed as soon as possible.

God will judge those outside of the church

As for those inside the church, God has given the church the authority to judge

So when there is sin in the church, the church should stand up and deal with it

The church ought to drive out those who do such evil things from the church

In order to keep the church holy, as well as hoping that those who are driven out will one day repent.

God has given the church the authority to deal with sin from within the church

This spiritual principle is also applicable to today’s churches

If someone in the church clearly commits a crime, and continues to commit the crime,

The church can’t just sit idly by

The principle in Matthew 18, verses 15 to 17 should be adopted

Firstly, one person should point out the mistake to the sinner first

If he doesn’t listen, take one or two others with you

If he still doesn’t listen, tell it to the church

If he still doesn’t listen, the sinner must be driven out of the church.

Because if sin is allowed to fester in the church

It will be like yeast in the dough

The influence of sin will eventually affect the whole church

Paul earnestly believes that the church is a holy gathering, redeemed by the blood of Jesus.

Paul believes that the Church must remain faithful and have a faith that’s right and true.

For this, Paul uses the parable of the old leave in order to emphasise that the saints of God should guard against the spread of sin

The saints must get rid themselves of of sin

We mustn’t let sin take a foothold.

I can’t help but think of myself

We are people, we are the temple of the Holy Spirit

So let’s decisively renounce the sins in our lives.

May our Lord help us.

Three questions this week:

1) Do you have any misunderstanding of the gospel of Jesus Christ?

2) What do you do when you see sin in the church?

3) What do you do when you discover sin in yourself?

The Corinthian Church is such a wretched Church!

At the beginning of this letter Paul said that they are called to be saints.

This is the kind of love that exists only in Christ.

God long to see repentance in every sinner

No matter what you have done, no matter what kind of sin you have committed

When you realise that it is sin, please turn back

Call on the name of Jesus Christ,

May grace and peace be with you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Let us pray,

“Loving Father, thank you for using these things that happened nearly two thousand years ago

To teach us and let us see the spiritual principles and meanings

So that we can beware of this sin

And not to fall into the same sin

We pray in the holy name of Jesus Christ

Amen”
