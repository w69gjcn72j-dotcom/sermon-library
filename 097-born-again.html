Where do we go for all our answers?

For many of us, if we have a question, we go to google.PPT

Google, of course, is a company but we’ve made it into a verb.

If we want to find out how to fix something, we ‘google’ it.

If we want to find out obscure historical fact, we ‘google’ it.

Nowdays, we don’t even need to sit at our computers; we ask our google smart speakers.

“Hey google, what’s the weather like?”

Today, we’re going to see a group of people who had all the answers about religion.

They were the human equivalents of Google of their day, in regard to all things religious.

They were the Pharisees.PPT

Asking the wrong questions

What are the qualifications of Jesus?

What are our qualifications?

Open up with me to John 3:1, “There was a man from the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.”

The Pharisees were a religious group who were known as separatists.

They wanted to separate themselves from the common people, from unclean people, from foreign people, so that they be holy for God.

They studied the Old Testament law very diligently to in order to be ceremonially and morally holy before God.

Their community would respect them as examples of what it meant to be a pious person; they were what a good Jew should strive to be.

If a Jew had a question about what it meant to follow God, they would go to the Pharisees and ask them.

After all, the Pharisees were the experts when it came to following God’s laws.

They would have all the answers!

When Jesus came onto the scene in Jerusalem, the Pharisees would’ve no doubt heard about him.

They might have heard it from one of the commoners in Jerusalem, “Mr Pharisee, Sir, there’s this man from Nazareth doing all these incredible signs in Jerusalem.

You might have also heard about his incident in the Temple last week, how he drove the sellers and the money changers away.

Who is he? What’s your opinion of him?”

And so, the Pharisees must have got together and analysed Jesus.

They would’ve got together their vast experience and encyclopaedic knowledge of the Old Testament and tried to figure out who Jesus is.

After they’ve drawn their conclusion, one of their own, Nicodemus went to see Jesus.PPT

Here’s what they thought about Jesus, in verse 2, “This man (Nicodemus) came to him at night and said, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God, for no one could perform these signs you do unless God were with him.”

Nicodemus has a lot of respect for Jesus; he calls Jesus Rabbi, which means teacher.

He is a Pharisee, someone who is highly respected as a teacher of God’s laws.

And he calls Jesus Rabbi, a Teacher.

But more than that, he calls Jesus a teacher who has come from God.

He knows that Jesus is more than just a teacher.

That’s his conclusion from all the signs that Jesus has been doing around Jerusalem.

Last week, we read in John chapter two, that the Jews in the Temple demanded Jesus to give them a sign.

Jesus tells them to destroy the temple and he’ll rebuild it in three days.

Jesus points them to the sign of his coming resurrection.

We then read that Jesus did many signs in Jerusalem and many people believed him but Jesus wouldn’t entrust himself to them because he had reservations about who they were on the inside.

He wasn’t convinced that they had understood the signs and believed him correctly.

There are people who saw what Jesus did and heard what Jesus’ said and believed correctly that He’s the Son of God.

There are others who also saw what Jesus did and heard what Jesus’ said and believed wrongly that he’s just a miracle worker.

How does Nicodemus believer about Jesus? Rightly or wrongly?

According to Nicodemus, Jesus must be a teacher from God, that’s his conclusion.

What’s his reasoning? For no one could perform these signs you do unless God were with him.

His reason is that unless God is with a person, no one can perform the signs Jesus that did.

According to his reasoning, Jesus is an authorised teacher from God because

Unless this PPT (God’s with Jesus), that PPT (Jesus’ signs authorising him as a teacher from God) can’t happen.

It seems to make sense; his logic seems sound.

But rather than agreeing with Nicodemus, Jesus offers a mild rebuke, using Nicodemus’ reasoning against him.

Look with me at verse 3, “Jesus replied, “Truly I tell you, unless someone is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.”

Strictly speaking, Nicodemus didn’t ask Jesus a question and so it seems strange that Jesus is replying.

But rather than responding to a question, Jesus is replying to Nicodemus’ conclusion about him.

From the signs that Jesus did, Nicodemus concluded that Jesus must be an authorised teacher from God.

But Jesus is saying that Nicodemus has got his conclusion wrong because the very basis of his reasoning is wrong.

Jesus turns the whole conversation around and says that unless Nicodemus is born again, he won’t be able to see who Jesus really is.

Here’s Jesus’ reasoning: Unless this PPT(someone is born again) that PPT(seeing the Kingdom of God in Jesus) can’t happen.

The basis of Nicodemus’ reasoning is that he thought he can tell from the signs and his vast knowledge of the Bible to determine who Jesus is.

However, Jesus turns it around and say that Nicodemus is not qualified to authoritatively determine who Jesus is.

It’s only the one who’s born again who can see the Kingdom of God; it’s only the one who’s born again who can answer the question about Jesus’ identity.

The problem with Nicodemus was the he was asking the wrong questions.

What do these signs tell me about Jesus? How is Jesus authorised to do these signs? How is Jesus qualified to do what he does?

What he should’ve been asking are questions about his own qualification.

Am I qualified to determine who Jesus is? Am I seeing the kingdom of God correctly?

Asking the wrong questions can be very revealing about the person who’s doing the asking.

For example, when you’re in a job interview, there are a few questions that you should not ask. PPT

Don’t ask, “How quickly can I get promoted?”

Why not? It shows that you’re not interested in this job.

Also, don’t ask, “Do you check references?”

Why not? It shows that you might have something to hide.

In Nicodemus’ case, his question might have been “What qualification does Jesus have for being a teacher of God’s people?”

“How can I determine that Jesus is right?”

We ask the same kind of questions too, don’t we?

“How do I know that Jesus is really who He says He is?”

“What qualifies him for his to make his claims over me?”

“What gives him the right to rule over my kingdom, my domain?”

These are the wrong questions to ask, because just like Nicodemus, we put ourselves in a position of authority to make a judgement call on Jesus.

Jesus is who we determine him to be.

But Jesus says, no, no. It’s not up to us to determine who he is, even if we honour him by calling him a teacher from God.

No, it’s the other way around, it’s us who needs to be qualified to see him as He is.

We are not qualified by default to see Jesus as he is.

To truly understand who Jesus is, we need to be born again.

Unless someone is born again…PPT

…He can’t see the Kingdom of God

a radical transformation

Look with me again at verse 3, “Truly I tell you, unless someone is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.”

The Kingdom of God (KOG) refers to God’s rule over His people.

Even though the phrase is not explicitly expressed as Kingdom of God, the idea is written all over the Old Testament.

In the Psalms we read that “The Lord reigns, he is robed in majesty” (Psalm 93:1)

In the prophets, we read that “the Lord himself will become King over the whole earth – the Lord alone, and his name alone.” (Zechariah 14:9)

The concept of the Kingdom of God is that one day, God will be the King of God’s people and they’ll be safe from harm.

The Jews, including Pharisees like Nicodemus, thought that with the exception of the very wicked, all Jews will enter God’s Kingdom.PPT

It was their birthright and the thing to do is to make sure they don’t sin really bad sins.

As long as they keep themselves as fairly good followers of God, then they’ll enter God’s kingdom, almost as if by default.

But according to Jesus, that’s not how you get into the Kingdom of God.

To enter the Kingdom of God, you need to be born again.

To be born again means that there’s a radical transformation; the person needs to live a new life.

It’s not just about getting rid of bad behaviours and bad habits and just trying to be a better person.

A radical change is needed.

It’s like someone who’s having Panadol for pain.PPT

When you ask them where the pain is and they tell you that it’s near the heart and the pain is travelling to the left side of the arm.

You tell them to go to the emergency department straight away and the person is diagnosed with having a heart attack.

The heart was diagnosed to be in a very bad shape, and they needed to have a heart transplant straight away.

Unless the person has a heart surgery, the person will die from a faulty heart.

In the same way, unless a person has a spiritual surgery on their heart, unless they’re born again, that person will not see God’s kingdom, they, too will die from a spiritually faulty heart.

Without being born again, even someone as knowledgeable as Nicodemus, with his vast knowledge of the Bible, would fail to see Jesus, the very personification of the Kingdom of God. PPT

The great reformer theologian John Calvin, once said, “By the term born again He means not the amendment of a part but the renewal of the whole nature. Hence it follows that there is nothing in us that is not defective.” Calvin

Jesus is saying that unless we have a radical change in our heart, unless there’s a renewal of our whole nature, we cannot see the Kingdom of God.

Nicodemus can’t believe what he’s hearing and so he says to Jesus, “How can anyone be born when he is old?” Nicodemus asked him. “Can he enter his mother’s womb a second time and be born?”

“It’s impossible” is basically what Nicodemus is saying.

How can our whole nature be renewed?

How are we born again?

Unless someone is born of water and the spirit…PPT

…He can’t enter the Kingdom of God

a Spirit-driven miracle

Look with me at verse 5, “Jesus answered, “Truly I tell you, unless someone is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. 6 Whatever is born of the flesh is flesh, and whatever is born of the Spirit is spirit.”

Jesus is here not talking about baptism.

You’re not born of water and the Spirit when you’re baptised.

Rather Jesus is talking about a deep cleansing on the inside, a cleansing of our hearts by the Spirit.

When you’re born again, you’re born of water and the Spirit; when you’re born again, there’s a deep cleansing inside that happens by the Spirit of God. PPT

We see this in a passage in the Book of Ezekiel 36:25-26, “25 I will also sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean. I will cleanse you from all your impurities and all your idols. 26 I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you; I will remove your heart of stone and give you a heart of flesh. 27 I will place my Spirit within you and cause you to follow my statutes and carefully observe my ordinances.”

God promises that one day, he himself will cleanse us and make us clean.

That’s the imagery of the water; water is used to wash away all our impurities.

And he’ll do that by giving us his Spirit, causing his Spirit to live within us.

He’ll give us a radical heart surgery; he’ll remove our heart of stone and replace it with a heart of flesh, so that we can obey his commandments.

To be born again means to be born of the water and the Spirit, that is, to be cleansed by the Holy Spirit of God.

Over the past few weeks, many people from all around the world suddenly got very, very concerned about cleanliness.

Why? Coronavirus.

I’m sure you’ve heard many stories about the extent that people go to keep themselves clean.

A few days ago, I saw an article about a journalist who visited Chinatown in Sydney to see the effects coronavirus had on the Chinese community. PPT

She went on a Tuesday afternoon, at lunchtime.

Usually, there would be plenty of people there but on that day, it was empty.

She then spoke her the taxi driver who told her that the passenger ahead of her was scrubbing his hands in sanitiser PPT

and had a face mask that he’s paid $40 for, though it probably was worth only $1.50 at most.

This passenger was very concerned that he would be infected with the coronavirus.

However, what would happen if that man had the coronavirus?

What would all the scrubbing do?

It doesn’t matter how many bottles of sanitiser he uses, it doesn’t matter how good his mask is, it’ll be all useless if he had already contracted the virus.

Why? It’s because if you are infected by the virus, you become a factory for the virus.

The way virus works is that it firstly infects a host cell.

Once the host cell is infected, it changes the programming of the cell to make it into a virus factory.

Viruses infect us at the deepest level, not just at the cellular level, but even smaller, at the nucleus of the cell, or at the heart of the cell.

You can scrub all you want but unless you treat the virus that’s at the heart of your cells, you’re just a big old virus maker.

That’s what sin is like.

Our hearts are like factories of sins.

We can try to cover up with good deeds, we can try to wash them away by our determination, but we can’t get rid of sin.

That’s because sin is in the deepest part of our being.

Just as we can’t wash viruses away from our cells, we can’t wash sins away from our hearts.

We can try, but even our best efforts are like filthy rags in God’s eyes.

As long as we have sin in our lives, we can’t enter into God’s kingdom.

That’s why we need the deep cleansing that comes only with the water and the Spirit.

When a person believes that Jesus died on the cross and rose from the dead, if they truly believe that the Lord Jesus is the Son of the Living God,

Then they’ll receive God’s Holy Spirit and be utterly cleansed from their sins.

It’s only as someone believes in God’s Son, Jesus Christ that they can receive the cleansing that comes with the Holy Spirit.

Just as whatever is born of the flesh is flesh, whatever born of the Spirit is spirit.

It’s only as we’re born again, by the power of the Holy Spirit, that we’ll begin a new life, cleansed from our sins.

In medicine, one way to treat viruses is to give patients antivirals.

These antivirals are drugs that change the make-up of the viruses themselves, making them ineffective in reproducing themselves.

When someone believes in Jesus, there’s a change that’s far more incredible, far more fundamental than what the antivirals can do.

When someone believes in Jesus, they are made qualified to see the Kingdom of God and to enter the Kingdom of God.

When they enter the Kingdom of God, they don’t need to fear death because they know that they have eternal life in the Lord Jesus Christ.

They become a new person and they become receptive to the things of God.

Before they believe in Jesus, before they’re cleansed by the Spirit, they remain deaf and blind to the things of the Kingdom of God.

Just like Nicodemus, they’ll miss it even if it’s right in front of them.

But after they believe in Jesus, after they’re cleansed by the Spirit, they become alive to the things of God.

They see the Kingdom of God; they’re awakened to the Kingdom of God.

Some of us might have heard this illustration before, but I think it’s helpful for our passage.PPT

In 1969, Dr Malcolm Sayer was treating patients who survived the encephalitis lethargica epidemic between 1917-1928.

These patients would be catatonic, that is, they appear to be dead, unresponsive to the outside world.

Dr Sayer started treating these patients with the drug L-Dopa and they started to be ‘awakened’ from their sleep.

It’s as if these patients have slept for years, decades even, and now, they’re awakened to a new life and a new time.

Dr Sayer’s experience has been turned into a film called ‘Awakenings’.

Sadly the effects of L-Dopa wore off and these patients went back to their catatonic state.

However, for a brief time, they were awakened and they were alive and they engaged with the world.

When someone believes in Jesus, they’re awakened to the Kingdom of God; they’re alive to the Kingdom of God.PPT

Unlike those treated with L-Dopa, Christians stay awake to the Kingdom of God.

That’s what Christians call eternal life; they will have everlasting life.

That life starts as soon as they believe in Jesus Christ.

When they die physically, the Bible describes them as going to sleep.

But when they open their eyes again, they will be awakened, and will continue living their new life in Jesus, their eternal life, forever.

This eternal life is not something that human beings can manipulate or manufacture.PPT

Jesus says that just as the wind blows where it pleases, so it is with everyone born of the Spirit.

We might see the effect of the Spirit on the person as we look at the life of the person, just as we see the effects of the wind.

But we can’t make the person alive; even the person can’t make themselves alive.

Only the Spirit can do that.

To be born again is a Spirit-driven miracle that only the Holy Spirit of God can achieve.

The only One with the answers PPT

Entering the Kingdom of heaven is an earthly matter

Only Jesus is qualified to speak on heavenly matters

After hearing Jesus’ explanation about what it means to be born again, Nicodemus asks, ‘how can these things be?’

Nicodemus simply can’t see what Jesus means by the new birth.

Listen to Jesus’ reply in verse 10-12, ““Are you a teacher of Israel and don’t know these things?” Jesus replied. 11 “Truly I tell you, we speak what we know and we testify to what we have seen, but you do not accept our testimony. 12 If I have told you about earthly things and you don’t believe, how will you believe if I tell you about heavenly things?”

Nicodemus, along with all the Pharisees, studied God’s word over and over again.

They should’ve been the ones to understand the absolute necessity for the Holy Spirit for cleansing and for a new birth.

They should have been the ones to explain how vital the new birth is to enter God’s kingdom.

But they have not understood God’s word.

How do we know? Because they have not accepted Jesus’ testimony.

They have not believed what Jesus’ said.

What Jesus said was foundational to understanding what it means to enter into the Kingdom of God.

If they don’t understand the basic of what it means to enter God’s Kingdom, something that happens while the person is still here on earth, how can they understand the things of heaven, the heavenly things?PPT

As impressive as the Pharisees are, they’re not the one with the answers.

The One with the answers is none other than Jesus Christ.

Look with me at the final verse of today’s passage: “13 No one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended from heaven —the Son of Man.”

Jesus, and Jesus alone is qualified to answer the questions that matter.PPT

Jesus and Jesus alone is qualified to answer our questions about life in the Kingdom of God, life in heaven, because he’s the One who had actually came from heaven.

He’s the One we turn to for answers.

We live in a world where we have many questions about who we are.

Over the past few months, we have experienced fire, we have experienced floods and we are experiencing pestilence in the form of a virus.

All over the world, there are many people suffering physical stress, mental stress and financial stress either directly or indirectly from the effects of the coronavirus outbreak.

And there are many questions that people may have.

What are our options? Will we be safe? How long will it last?

These are all valid questions but ultimately, they are not the most important questions.

Ultimately, our questions need to be right ones, they need to go back to the very basic: Am I born again? Am I in the Kingdom of God? Have I been thoroughly and utterly cleansed by the Holy Spirit?

For at the end of the day, being alive, being awakened to the reality of the Kingdom of God is far more important, far more wonderful, than knowing that I’m safe from the coronavirus.

In an age of anxiety, in an age of uncertainty, this is the message that Christians have.

And so, brothers and sisters, don’t shy away from sharing the gospel, especially in a time like this.

It’s especially during this time of hopelessness that God wants you to bring hope to a people utterly lost.

When you get an opportunity to share the good news of Jesus, do it!

Don’t be afraid of who they are, or where they’ve been or whether they’re healthy or not.

They need to hear that there’s hope in the Good news of Jesus Christ!

In a few weeks’ time, we’re going to bring God’s good news of hope, God’s good news of new birth, to the people of Kogarah.

Will you join me and the Moore College team on this work?

Will you pray for me and the Moore College team as we do this work?

Here are the three questions for this week: PPT

Am I still trying to see whether Jesus is qualified to fit in my kingdom or am I trying to see whether I’m qualified to be in his kingdom?

What are the signs in my life that I’ve been utterly cleansed by the Holy Spirit?

When was the last time I’ve prayed for the Spirit to work in someone important in my life?

Brothers and sisters, as we come to Jesus Christ, let’s take a long and meaningful look at the kind of questions we’re asking him.

What do our questions reveal about ourselves?

Do we they show that we trust in Jesus as our Lord and Saviour, that He is the Ultimate Judge,

Or do they show that we still consider ourselves to be in a position to make a judgement call on Jesus?

Let’s humble ourselves before Jesus, believing what he says about himself, believing that it’s only through him that we can be born again, born of water, born of the Spirit.

And as a people born again, we can see and enter into the Kingdom of God.
