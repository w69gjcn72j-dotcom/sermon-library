God is love - The triune God who has always loved

Passage: John 14:18-26

Big Idea: The God who is Three in One, in whom each Person of the Godhead loves each other perfectly, loves and is loved by those who obey Jesus.

The Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit loves one another perfectly

To love Jesus is to obey Jesus’ words

The Father and the Son, by the Spirit, make their home with the obedient.

Last week, we began our sermon series on ‘God is love’ by talking about the language of love.

It’s through the Holy Scripture, God’s letter of love, that we know that God loves us with his faithful love.

It’s through the Holy Scripture that we know that God loves us in his Beloved Son Jesus.

This week, we’ll continue the series by thinking about the Triune God who has always loved.

As Christians we love talking about God’s love, we love singing about God’s love.

We read about what God has done for his people, and how he’s sent His Son to the world to save us and we go, “Wow! Isn’t that amazing! Isn’t God good!”

However, there are people who read the very same words as us, and they don’t respond in the same way.

They read it, and they don’t find joy; they read it and they don’t see love.

Why do some people love Jesus while others don’t?

That’s what we’re going to explore in today’s passage, and it all starts with coming to know the Triune God who has always loved.

If you have your Bible there, please open up to John 14, beginning from verse 18.

We’re going to see three things in the Bible today:

The Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit loves one another perfectly

To love Jesus is to obey Jesus’ words

The Father and the Son, by the Spirit, make their home with the obedient.

Before we look into God’s word, let’s pray, “Father, thank you for revealing yourself to us through your Word, the Bible. As we hear from your word, please help us by your Spirit to see Jesus more clearly, in whose name we pray, Amen.”

1. God speaks words of faithful love

Throughout history, God has revealed himself to be a Person.

Not Person in terms of being a Human, but a Person in terms of being someone who has an identity, who has a name.

When Moses asked God for his name in Exodus 3, God replied, “I AM WHO I AM. This is what you are to say to the Israelites: I AM has sent me to you.”

The word that was translated ‘I AM WHO I AM’ is YHWH.

It’s a word that the Israelites didn’t pronounce because they considered it too holy.

Most scholars think that it’s pronounced Yahweh.

In our English Bible translations, the word Yahweh is usually translated as the word “LORD” in capital letters.

In our Chinese Bible translations, the word Yahweh is usually translated as “耶和华”.

The God of the Bible is a Person, a divine Person, with a divine name.

He is a divine person with divine characteristics.

This is how God describes himself in Exodus 34:6-7, “The Lord—the Lord is a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love and truth, maintaining faithful love to a thousand generations, forgiving iniquity, rebellion, and sin. But he will not leave the guilty unpunished, bringing the consequences of the fathers’ iniquity on the children and grandchildren to the third and fourth generation.”

God is a not a set of laws or philosophies or principles.

God is a Person, a divine Person.

When a Christian says he believes in God, it’s not belief in an impersonal religion.

When a Christian says he believes in God, he’s saying that believes in a Personal, Divine Person.

It’s a little like this.

When I say I have faith in the ground, I’m saying that this ground is solid, and will support me when I stand on it.

I have faith in it.

But I don’t have any personal relationship to it; I don’t pray to it to protect me from earthquakes, and it doesn’t speak to me.

When I say I have faith in God, I’m saying that I trust him as a Divine Person.

I pray to God and I expect God to be able to hear me.

He may or may not answer my prayers; that’s his choice, that’s his freedom.

And he has that freedom because He’s not a machine but a Person, a divine Person.

We believe in a God that we can trust, a God who we can have a relationship with.

Over time, God has continued to reveal more of himself to us.

The greatest revelation of God came when He sent Jesus to the world.

When Philip, one of Jesus’ disciples, asked Jesus to show them the Father, Jesus responded, ““Have I been among you all this time and you do not know me, Philip? The one who has seen me has seen the Father. How can you say, ‘Show us the Father’? Don’t you believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me?” John 14:9-10a.

Jesus says that the Father is in him and that he is in the Father.

They are so close that when you see Jesus, you see the Father; when you hear Jesus, you hear the Father.

The way to come to know God the Father is now through God the Son, Jesus Christ.

Jesus is not just one way to know the Father, he’s the only way to know the Father,

For Jesus himself has said, “I am the Way, the Truth and the Life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”

And so, there’s God the Father, and you can know him only through God the Son.

However, since then, Jesus has died on the cross, rose from the dead, and ascended to the Father’s side.

How will we know him now? How do we come to him?

We now come to him because there’s God the Holy Spirit.

After Jesus ascended to heaven, He sent the Holy Spirit to his disciples.

Jesus calls the Holy Spirit “Counsellor”, whom the Father has sent in his name, to teach the first disciples all things and to remind them of everything Jesus has told them.

And so, with the help of the Holy Spirit, the first disciples taught according to Jesus’ words and they wrote down Jesus’ words.

It’s through their words, as recorded in the Bible, that we come to know Jesus.

With the help of the Holy Spirit, the words of Jesus continues to be taught, even to this day.

And so, who is God? God is Triune.

As an ancient creed has taught us: The God we worship is one God in Trinity, and Trinity in unity. (我們等敬拜一体三位，而三位一体之神。)

Our God is God one God in Three Persons, God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Spirit.

We don’t believe in three gods. We don’t believe in tritheism.

And we don’t believe that there are three parts to one God.

The Father, the Son, the Holy spirit – each Divine Persons are fully God but they are not separate gods.

We don’t believe that there’s one God who comes to us in three modes.

We don’t believe in modalism either.

A common way to describe Trinity is water.

Some people say that water has three states, Solid (ice), liquid (running water) and gas (steam).

They say that’s what God is like, God is sometimes the Father, sometimes the Son, sometimes the Spirit, depending on the situation.

But that’s wrong!

The Bible teaches us that each Divine person have their own distinct personhood.

The Father loves the Son and the Son loves the Father.

While it’s true that you can love yourself, the language that Jesus uses makes sense only if he’s talking about another Person.

The Father sends the Holy Spirit, and the Son sends the Holy Spirit.

You don’t send yourself away to do things, you send someone else.

They are distinct persons, and not just different modes of the one person.

How does the Bible account for the fact that there’s One God but there are Three distinct Divine persons?

The Bible uses the idea of mutually indwelling to describe the relationship between the Divine Persons.

God the Father lives in God the Son; God the Son lives in God the Father.

And God the Spirit lives in both God the Father and God the Son, because He’s the Spirit of both the Father and the Son.

The relationship between God is beyond the power of our human words.

This is where our human words find their limit.

But from the little that we do know, we see a God who has known perfect love for all eternity.

All divine Persons within the Godhead love each other perfectly before time began and will continue to do so long after time ends.

They had no need to go beyond themselves to look for love; the love the Divine Persons of the Godhead have for one another is sufficient.

As humans, we do see fragments of love in our world.

Sometimes we see love in our families and among our friends.

Sometimes, we see love even among neighbours and strangers.

But while love is a good thing, our love is often tainted, with greed, or hidden motives.

We often love others because we of what they can offer us; we love because we love ourselves first.

It’s a tainted love.

But the love between the Persons of the Godhead is far superior to our kind of love.

To know what love truly is, we have to come back to the love between the Persons of the Godhead.

We need to find this love that is truly love.

2. To love Jesus is to obey Jesus’ words

We thank God that as images of God, we were made to relate to God like no other creatures can.

We can love God like no other can.

We were made to love God as persons, human persons, made in his own image. How do we love God? With our inferior and tainted love?

No, God teaches us how to love him with this kind of love, the superior, deeper, eternal love.

Even though we don’t know how to love, God shows us how.

Even though we can’t in and of ourselves love as God loves, he enables us so that we can.

How? Through the words of Jesus.

Look with me at John 14:21, “The one who has my commands and keeps them is the one who loves me. And the one who loves me will be loved by my Father. I also will love him and will reveal myself to him.”

Jesus had just told his disciples he will be leaving soon.

He said that the disciples will see him but the world will not.

Jesus is here referring to his resurrection.

When Jesus rises from the dead, they will see him and they will understand.

They will also know that Jesus is living in the Father and the Father is living in Him.

They will begin to see that God is a Triune God.

The disciples will love Jesus, the Father will love the disciples, and the Son will love the disciples.

They will know what it is to be loved by God and to love God.

How will they know? By keeping Jesus’ words.

Many years ago, a young man from my old Church proposed to a girl in our Young Adults group.

Some of you know them, the young man is Ken and the girl is Sienna.

On the day of his proposal, Ken gave a book of instructions to a friend to give to Sienna.

Sienna had to follow the friend to different places around Sydney.

They weren’t just random places, they were places which were highlights of their relationships.

It had pictures of where they’ve been, pictures of adventures that they’ve shared.

It even has a picture of the McDonald’s where they had one of their first meals together.

And so, Sienna followed these friends to the different places.

When Sienna got to the final place, Ken was there, waiting, and ready to propose to Sienna.

It’s romantic, and frankly, it sets a really high bar for the boys!

Jesus loves those who are his own, with a love that’s even greater than Ken’s love for Sienna.

And He wants his own to love him, not with an inferior love,

but with the love that he shared with the Father and the Spirit for all eternity.

Jesus invites his own to this exclusive love that he has shared with the Father and the Spirit.

He wants them to be loved by His Father too.

He wants his own to feel and to experience the love he’s always known.

The way he invites us to know this love, to know his love, is his words.

We know that we love Jesus when we keep and obey his words.

Jesus has given us his book of instructions on how to love him.

If we love him, we will obey him, because we know that he loves us!

When he says, “come, follow me”, we obey because you love him.

We will realise that we know about God the Father, God the Son and God the Spirit, when we obey the words of Jesus.

We know that we are loved by God, and that we love God, when we obey the words of Jesus.

Our obedience to Jesus’ words is evidence of God’s love in us.

Our obedience to Jesus’ words shows us that our love is based on the love that Jesus himself had shown.

Just as Jesus loved the Father and obeyed the Father, we too, will obey Jesus if we love Jesus.

Just as the Father has revealed himself to the Son because He loves him, the Son will also reveal himself to us, because he loves us.

When we obey the words of Jesus and we see him by faith, we will know that the love of God is active in our lives.

When we obey the words of Jesus, even when it’s at great cost to us, we will know that we love him more than life itself.

We will know love that is truly love.

A few nights ago, I heard about African Churches who refused aid from Churches from another country.

We’re talking about millions of dollars that can do much good for the people of the country.

However, these African brothers and sisters refused help because these so-called Christians taught falsely in God’s name.

They taught lies in God’s name.

Our African brothers and sisters would rather obey Jesus than to receive the aid that they desperately need.

They love Jesus more than life itself.

I’m so glad that on Good Friday, our Church was able to raise $315 for our African brothers and sisters.

Please continue to support and help them as they keep trusting in Jesus.

But coming back to ourselves, what would we do if we were in their shoes?

Would we choose our own financial security, our own well-being over obedience to the words of Jesus?

Would we choose peace and avoid conflicts instead of obeying Jesus?

If we choose our security, our idea of peace, instead of obedience to the words of Jesus, we will show that we don’t love Jesus.

If we disobey Jesus and his words, we will miss out on the security and peace that truly counts.

3. The Father and the Son, by the Spirit, make their home with those who obey

Another of Jesus’ disciples, Judas (not Iscariot), asked why it is that Jesus doesn’t make himself known to the world and only choose to show himself to the disciples.

Listen to Jesus’ answer in verse 23, ““If anyone loves me, he will keep my word. My Father will love him, and we will come to him and make our home with him.”

Our knowledge and our love of Jesus go together.

You can’t know Jesus without loving Jesus.

And you can’t love Jesus without keeping his words.

Jesus will only reveal himself to those who love him, those who keep his words.

When that happens, God the Father and God the Son makes their home with him.

They will live with him; His home will be their home.

A few weeks ago, quite a few families among us helped the Rymans (Steve, Jennifer and Mikey) moved into the area.

It was a massive effort!

There were a lot of packing and preparing and moving!

But eventually, we got there.

They now live in a new home, and they will stay here for a while.

Ironically, they’re not at home but away this week for a holiday.

We look forward to seeing them again next week!

God makes his home in the person who keeps the words of Jesus, who loves Jesus.

The Father will love Him and Jesus will love him.

They will experience the love that God has for the Son and the Son has for the Father from the very beginning.

But the one who does not love Jesus won’t obey his words.

They will reject the words of Jesus and since Jesus’ words are the Father’s words, they are rejecting the Father’s words as well.

By rejecting the words of Jesus and the words of the Father, they have rejected God himself.

The promise of the Father’s love won’t apply to them.

If you don’t obey Jesus’ words, you won’t know him, you won’t love him.

How do we know Jesus’ words?

We know Jesus’ words only by the work of the Holy Spirit.

The Holy Spirit is the Counsellor whom the Father had sent in the Son’s name. The Spirit reminded and taught the disciples everything that Jesus said.

He’s the one who helped the disciples record the words of Jesus and taught them to others.

He’s the One God sent into our hearts so that we can pray “Abba, Father”.

Even now, the Holy Spirit helps us understand the words of Jesus so that we can come to know Jesus.

When we come to know Jesus, believe in him, the Father and the Son, comes to live in our hearts and minds by the Holy Spirit.

It’s by the Holy Spirit that we can come to know and believe the words of Jesus, and so come to know God.

It’s by the Holy Spirit that we obey the words of Jesus.

It’s by the Spirit that we come to know Jesus, and to love Jesus above all things.

What does loving Jesus above all things look like.

Let me finish off with a momentous event that happened on Friday night.

To understand how important it was, we need to go back almost 500 years to the beginning of the Church of England.

The Church of England started off in England.

As the English influence grew, the Church of England followed.

You can find the Church of England in many countries around the world.

While they’re called the Church of England, each country, or each province, if they are more than one country, have their own leaders.

They are not under the rule of the Church of England.

Even so, many recognise that the Church of England is the Mother Church.

All Anglican Churches around the world grew from the Church of England.

It has a wonderful and fascinating history and I hope you get a chance to find out more about it.

However, lately the Church of England, the one in England has lost its way. Many of the bishops affirmed teachings contrary to the Bible.

And so, many Anglican churches from outside of England formed GAFCON (Global Anglican Future Conference) in response.

Last week, GAFCON had a conference in Kigali, Rwanda

On Friday night, Anglican leaders representing 85% of Anglicans in the world made an important statement.

They called for Church of England to repent.

Here’s an excerpt from their statement:

“We long for this repentance but until they repent, our communion with them remains broken.

We consider that those who refuse to repent have abdicated their right to leadership within the Anglican Communion, and we commit ourselves to working with orthodox Primates and other leaders to reset the Communion on its biblical foundations.”

Together, these leaders have rejected the leadership of the Church of England in the Anglican Communion

Our own regional Bishop, Bishop Michael Stead, was the chairman of the committee who wrote the statement.

It was a very, very sad thing to do.

The Mother Church was wrong in rejecting the words of Jesus.

The Mother Church had turned away from the Bible.

The Mother Church no longer follows Jesus’ words, she no longer loves him as she should.

It was very hard for the millions of the Anglicans around the world to reject their Mother Church, their home Church.

However, they know that their true Home is where those who belong to Jesus love him and obey him.

It’s those who love and obey Jesus’ words who are loved by God.

For where the words of Jesus are heard and followed, that’s where Father and Son make their home, by the Holy Spirit.

Why do some people love Jesus more than others?

It’s because they have heard and obeyed Jesus’ words.

They obey Jesus because they love Jesus.

And they love Jesus because Jesus first loved them.

Friends, we serve one God in Trinity and Trinity in Unity.

We serve God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Spirit.

We serve the God who is love, who loves each Person of the Godhead perfectly.

Enter into God’s love, by keeping the words of Jesus.

Here are the three questions for this week.

1. In what way do I falsely separate my love of Jesus from my obedience to Jesus?

2. When was my love for Jesus put to the test last week?

3. Is there anything in your life more important than loving and being loved by God?

Friends, if our answer to the last question is yes, then we haven’t really known or love God yet.

Come to know God, come to know Jesus, obey him, for there is nothing in the world, nothing in all universe, more precious, more beautiful, than being at home with God, loving and being loved by the Father, Son and the Holy Spirit.

Enter into God’s love, by keeping the words of Jesus.

Let’s pray, “Our heavenly Father, thank you for loving us in Your Son Jesus Christ. Please forgive us for the times when we have rejected his words, when we have not obeyed Him. By your Spirit, incline our hearts to obey and treasure the words of Jesus, so that we may know that you love us and grow in our knowledge of your love. Help us to keep the words of Jesus so that we may love you, and grow in our love for you. For Jesus’ sake, Amen.”
