As a Church, we thank God that we have many children and youth ministries here throughout the week.

On Sundays we have KOG Kids and KOG Youth, on Mondays, Thursdays, and Fridays we have SRE and on Friday afternoons, we have KOGWorx at the Children Centre.

It’s great to have so many kids and youth ministries.

I thank God for all the teachers we have!

But as teachers, we know that sometimes, kids will push the boundaries; they will test your limits!

I still remember in my old Church when we would have a Mandarin service and all the parents would go to the service and leave the kids to the English teenagers to look after.

I was one of those teenagers being rostered on one particular Sunday.

I had no training, no teaching material, a few toys and I had to look after every kid at church, from infants to primary, all at once.

It was crazy! The kids were running around, and I was trying to stop them.

I told them to stop playing rough with other kids, but they just kept going.

They kept pushing my envelope and I was wondering what my breaking point will be.

I think they were thinking the same thing, “How far can we push Mr David?”

I was really glad when Church finally finished, and the parents returned to pick them up.

And so, parents, when you pick up your children later, remember to thank the teachers!

Now, of course, it’s not only children who know how to push the limits, but adults too.

I’ve seen some adults keep pushing to see how far they can get away with doing something wrong.

Wow! If I thought kids were bad, the adults can be a lot worse!

Some adults just keep doing the wrong thing despite you telling them that it’s wrong.

Others refuse to do the right thing no matter how many times you tell them it’s right.

It’s frustrating!

Now multiply that by a couple of billions of times and you’ll get a glimpse of how frustrating it must be for God.

God has told us what we needed to do and what we mustn’t do, but we keep ignoring him.

We keep pushing God’s envelope, as it were.

Will God have a limit?

Will we ever go too far?

Will God ever say, enough is enough?

That’s what we ‘re going to explore in today’s passage.

Please open up to Genesis chapter 6, beginning from verse 1.

We’ll see three things:

Let’s pray before we begin, “Father, please open our heart to your words and let them lead us to Jesus, in whose name we pray, Amen.”

Planet Wickedness

In Genesis chapter five, we read that ten generations have now passed from the time of Adam to the time of Noah.

By Noah’s time, there were many, many people on earth.

Look with me at verses 1-2, “When mankind began to multiply on the earth and daughters were born to them, the sons of God saw that the daughters of mankind were beautiful, and they took any they chose as wives for themselves.”

Who are these sons of God and daughters of men?

Aren’t they all sons and daughters of men?

Aren’t these sons of God also the sons of men?

There are some people who say that the sons of God are the godly men, coming from the line of Seth, the third son of Adam.

And the daughters of men are the daughters of ungodly men, coming from the line of Cain, the eldest son of Adam, who killed Abel, Adam’s second son.

If that was the case, why were the sons of God described as men who lust / gawk after the beautiful daughters of men.

That doesn’t sound very godly at all!

No, since the ancient times, the Jews, along with many Christians after them, understood the reference to the sons of God as a reference to heavenly beings.

Not good beings, but heavenly beings who long for human women.

We see a reference to the sons of God in Job 1:6, “One day the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan also came with them.”

The sons of God were heavenly, non-human beings who came to present themselves, along with Satan, before God.

From the Old Testament point of view, the sons of God in Job were like the sons of God in Genesis 6, heavenly beings.

These ‘sons of God’ were fallen angels, who long for bodies.

They are spiritual beings but they want to be in a body.

Jesus gives a hint of that in Luke 11:24-26,

“When an unclean spirit comes out of a person, it roams through waterless places looking for rest, and not finding rest, it then says, ‘I’ll go back to my house that I came from.’ Returning, it finds the house swept and put in order. Then it goes and brings seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they enter and settle down there. As a result, that person’s last condition is worse than the first.”

The unclean spirit, a heavenly being, finds rest in bodies.

They possess a body in order to find rest.

That’s probably what happened in Genesis 6.

Fallen angels, the sons of God, possessed the sons of men.

These demon-possessed men saw the beauty of the daughters of men and wanted to take them as their wives.

Now, notice that there were no indications that the sons of God forced the daughters to become their wives.

In the ancient times, as still in many cultures today, daughters were given in marriage by the father.

Without the father’s permission, the daughters couldn’t be given.

And so, it was most likely that the fathers willingly gave their daughters away to men possessed by the sons of God.

Why would they do that?

It’s not explicit but it probably had something to do with their desire for long life, even eternal life.

What the men and women at that time might have thought was that if the sons of God, heavenly beings, married the daughters of men, they can achieve long life and even immortality.

But God’s put an end to that.

In verse 3, we read God’s response to these unions between the sons of God and the daughters of men. “And the Lord said, “My Spirit will not remain with mankind forever, because they are corrupt. Their days will be 120 years.”

God’s spirit will not remain with them forever; they won’t have eternal life.

Instead of the average of about 900 years, their lifespan will cut to 120 years.

And it gets worse.

Look with me at verse 5, “When the Lord saw that human wickedness was widespread on the earth and that every inclination of the human mind was nothing but evil all the time”

When God finished creating the world, he saw all that he had made and it was good, very good.

But now, when God saw the world, he saw human wickedness all over his earth.

The world he has made had turned from good to utterly wicked.

Humans multiplied, as he commanded them to, but as they multiply, so did their wickedness.

Their wickedness covered the whole earth; it was extensive.

It was also intensive.

Every thought of the human mind was nothing but evil all the time.

Every imagination, every plan, going through the person’s brain was evil, all of the time.

That’s intense!

Here we see humanity descending to its deepest depth.

They were more satanic than Godlike.

They were closer to hell than they were to heaven.

Every thought was evil all the time, and they’ve covered our planet with their wickedness.

That was the world of Noah, but is it our world also?

Let’s play a game to find out, shall we?

It’s called Devil’s Advocate.

It’s a character that a person plays, usually a bad character, to see if we’re ready for what the bad person, or the devil, might suggest.

Ready?

If you’re the devil, what would you do to bring humanity to its lowest depths again?

Someone once said, “The greatest trick the Devil ever pulled was convincing the world he didn’t exist.”

If you can convince your enemy that you don’t exist, that you’re not a threat, then you can easily win over them.

What else would you do?

You would influence people and to get them to start hating God, convince them that religion is the opiate of the people, that Church is bad for you.

You will tell people that they don’t need God in their lives; you’ll tell them not forbid kids from learning about God.

That’s what you’ll do if you were the devil, wouldn’t you?

And that’s exactly what’s happening in our world.

But it gets worse, the devil has gone more than staying in the shadows.

He’s now out in the public, celebrated by thousands, if not millions.

A few weeks ago, on the Grammy music award, one of the key performances of the show was Unholy, by Sam Smith, who dressed as the devil.

Satan has convinced the world to think that he’s cool.

It’s almost as outrageous as the men and women in Genesis 6 who thought that literally sleeping with the devil is a good idea.

Our world today is running straight into hell, just as it did back in Genesis 6.

Just how low can we go?

Will God be forever patient with us?

Is there a limit to God’s patience? Will we ever reach a point where God will say “enough is enough.”

Finding favour in an extinction Level event

In response to the wickedness of mankind in Genesis 6, God initiated an Extinction Level event.

Look with me at verses 6-7, “the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and he was deeply grieved. 7 Then the LORD said, “I will wipe mankind, whom I created, off the face of the earth, together with the animals, creatures that crawl, and birds of the sky—for I regret that I made them.”

God will wipe mankind away; he will bring about a complete destruction on the face of the earth.

It’s like this:

See this dirty plate?

It’s gross, it’s dirty.

It needs to be wiped clean.

This is what God will do to the world.

The world was filled to the brim with wickedness and corruption.

It was all bad.

The wickedness of mankind has crossed a threshold that warranted our extinction.

And God regretted, regretted that he had made man on the earth, and he was deeply grieved.

God had pain in his heart because he had made man.

When the Bible described God’s regret, it’s not regret in the sense that he changed his mind,

or in the sense that he made a mistake.

Rather it was regret in the sense that He was saddened by the fact that the wickedness of his people had reached such a point that he had to destroy them.

He was not a cold God who simply destroyed a world that was ruined.

He was a loving God whose heart ached at the thought of destroying human kind.

For God takes no pleasure in anyone’s death.

And as humankind, we crossed that point, the point that warranted our death and destruction.

Mankind had ruined the world with our wickedness and now the world, along with mankind, will become ruins.

And God’s judgement is fair.

God’s destruction of the world showed the state of the world as it truly was.

It was morally ruined in God’s face and so God will now bring it to ruins physically before his face.

And yet, in the middle of the impending doom, we saw this glimmer of hope in verse 8,

“Noah, however, found favour with the Lord.”

God was pleased with Noah.

Noah was a righteous man; he did what was right when everyone around him were doing evil.

Noah was blameless. He wasn’t sinless but he lived his life with such integrity that you can’t blame him for what he’s done.

And Noah walked with God.

He was in a good relationship with God.

There were people walking with demons all around him, but Noah walked with God.

Noah had faith in God, he trusted God.

We’ll see this in latter parts of the Bible, but it’s precisely because he trusted God that brought about him being righteous before God.

To save Noah from the extinction level flood, God told Noah to build an ark.

It was a large by ancient standard.

Every creature under heaven as about to perish, all life on earth was about to perish,

but those with Noah will be safe.

Look with me at verse 18, “But I will establish my covenant with you,a and you will enter the ark with your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives.”

God made a covenant with Noah; God Promised to keep his family of eight safe.

God also wanted to keep some a remnant of the animals safe.

Look with me at verses 19-20, “9 You are also to bring into the ark two of all the living creatures, male and female, to keep them alive with you. 20 Two of everything—from the birds according to their kinds, from the livestock according to their kinds, and from the animals that crawl on the ground according to their kinds—will come to you so that you can keep them alive.”

There were two of all everything; but something’s missing from the list.

Can you see what it is?

It’s fish!

Noah didn’t need to bring fish in because they’re safe in the waters of the flood!

How did Noah respond?

He obeyed; his faith in God was put into action.

He did what God had commanded him.

It took him one hundred years to build the ark.

Imagine trying to explain to his neighbours what he was doing.

“What are you building, Noah?” “An ark” “What for” “For the Extinction Level event flood that God’s bringing to the earth.”

“A what now?” “God will destroy the earth because of your wickedness”

It would’ve sounded strange then just as it does today.

Noah would’ve been mocked and ridiculed for 100 years as he built the ark.

Besides his family, there was literally no one else in the world who believed him.

He was very much in the minority!

But Noah kept building, and from 2 Peter 2:5, we read that he was a preached and so he would’ve kept preaching in the face of fierce opposition.

Why? Because he trusted God, and he walked with God.

He obeyed God even when everyone around him didn’t.

Last week, Wing Khong, an owner of a Kwik Kopy Printing store, refused to print an event promoting a homosexual event.

He said that to do so would be against his religious beliefs.

Now he said this in as week leading to World Pride month.

And so, he received a lot of criticisms, a lot of hate, a lot of unwanted media attention for his stand.

By refusing to print this, he might have lost a lot of business.

It would hurt both his bottom line and his reputation.

But he was a brave man; he would rather obey God than making a few extra dollars.

The business was not worth him disobeying God.

Would you stand firm in the face of fierce opposition?

Would you trust God, even if it means financial ruin?

Would you obey when it costs you something?

That’s what Noah and Wing did, obeying God, living a blameless life, rather than following the ways of a corrupted world.

3. How to be blameless in a corrupted world

Friends, we are living in a corrupted world.

But is it bad enough for God to warrant an extinction level even again?

Absolutely!

The world we live in today will end.

We’ve seen that warning again and again in the Bible.

The world is in ruins and is heading once again to ruins.

And the signs are loud and clear!

We’ve seen how corrupted the society has become; we’ve seen how our society prefers to the ways of the evil one rather than the way of God.

We’ve seen wars and rumors of wars.

We’ve seen famines and pandemics.

These are the signs of a corrupted world, heading for destruction.

But these have been happening for a long time; but the end hasn’t come yet.

Are there signs that tell us when the world will actually end?

What will happen on the day, on the actual day, when God brings about an Extinction Level Event?

I can tell you exactly what will happen on the day.

Jesus has told us what will happen on the actual day.

Are you ready?

Open your Bible to Matthew 24:37-39, “As the days of Noah were, so the coming of the Son of Man will be. 38 For in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day Noah boarded the ark.a 39 They didn’t know until the flood came and swept them all away. This is the way the coming of the Son of Man will be.”

Has anyone eaten today? That’s what’s going to happen on the final day.

Who will be going to a wedding in the next few weeks? That’s what’s going to happen on the final day.

The final day, the Day of Judgement, the Day of the Lord Jesus, will come on a normal looking day.

Just as the people in Noah’s day didn’t know until it was too late, the majority of the people in our world won’t know the judgement of God, until it’s too late.

Except this time, God won’t be destroying the world by water but by fire (2 Peter 3:6-7)

But thanks be to God, just as God provided rescue the last time, God has provided rescue this time also.

Last time, God saved the world through providing for Noah and his family.

This time, God saves the world through providing his Son, Jesus Christ.

Noah has found favour with God

We can now find favour with God in and only in Jesus Christ.

Those who trust in Jesus, who believed that Jesus has come and died for them, are washed clean.

God has saved those who trust in Jesus by making them even more righteous and more blameless than even Noah.

And as people washed clean by God in Jesus, we’re to live blameless lives, lives that reflect God’s holiness.

And so, if you’re a Christian don’t walk in darkness, don’t walk in the ways of demons anymore.

Walk with Jesus, only he can take you to safety.

If you’re not a Christian yet, then please go to Jesus and be washed.

Do it before it’s too late!

This is why we exist here as a Church.

We’re here to be the place where sinners come to hear the good news of Jesus, trust in him and so be made clean in God’s sight.

That’s why we as a Church also go out to tell the Gospel.

Like Noah, we proclaim righteousness.

And like Noah’s ark, our Church is a rescue boat.

If anything, our Church is like a lifeboat.

When we see people drowning in sin, we want to reach out to save them.

We want to bring as many people in the lifeboat as we can; the broken, the weak, the abandoned, the outsider.

We’re not a cruise but a lifeboat.

We don’t exist for the comfort of our members, but to train lifesavers who will save lives.

It’s all hands on deck, everyone is to do their bit to help with the rescue.

Just as there are all kinds of animals in the ark, the Church is for all types of people, whether you’re Chinese or Australian, Indian or Italian, young or old, the Church is built to rescue you and to rescue those who are not in the lifeboat yet.

We exist to save as many people as possible, for God’s judgement, God’s extinction level even is coming.

Are you ready?

The world is heading for extinction; head to Christ for salvation.

The Titanic was a very big ship that was so well-designed that it was described as unsinkable.

Sadly, a little after two hours into her maiden voyage, the ship struck an iceberg and sank.

Friends, living our life without Jesus is like living on the Titanic before it sank.

We could be living in luxury, we could be comfortable, we could really enjoy ourselves, but we are heading towards a disaster.

Living our busy lives without Jesus is like shuffling chairs on a Titanic.

Don’t stay on the Titanic, get into the lifeboat.

Get on to the lifeboat where it will be safe.

When you’re on the lifeboat, do your bit to bring others to safety.

At Church, we have a Giftedness Team, that helps each one of us think about how we’re to use the gifts God’s given us to help with the rescue.

Over time, the Giftedness Team and I will share the different trainings we have to help us share the gospel, to help us read the Bible, to help us disciple others.

There are many people around us who are on the way to hell, unless they hear about the good news of Jesus.

As Church, we want well-trained lifesavers who will get on with the mission of rescue.

Pray that God will use you in his mission to rescue the lost.

These are the three questions for this week:

Have I been following the devil’s advice, or have I been obeying God?

When was a time when you stood firm for what God’s commanded, despite the cost?

When I come to Church, do I come as a cruise passenger or a lifesaver?

Friends, God has had enough with the sins of the world.

He will bring the corrupted world to extinction.

Don’t follow the corrupted crowd, but walk with the blameless Christ.

Let’s pray,

“Father, please forgive us for our ignorance at the devil’s schemes. Please forgive us when we follow the corrupted ways of the world, the devil and the flesh instead of your way through Jesus. Father, renew us the urgency of bring the good news of Jesus to the lost, for without him, all would be lost. By your Spirit, strengthen us, revive us, that we might be blameless in our walk with you. Grant this through our Lord Jesus Christ, who lives and reigns with you and the Holy Spirit, one God, forever and ever, Amen.”
