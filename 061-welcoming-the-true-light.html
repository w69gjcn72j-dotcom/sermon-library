Sunday sermon 27-6-2021

Welcoming the true light and being a disciple of Christ

Passage: Matthew 4:12-25

introduction:

I am very happy to share the message of the Bible with you,

which is the good news of an abundant life.

I like this song very much:

“The most beautiful blessing in this life.

The most beautiful blessing in this life is this:

It is to be able to know, believe and follow the Lord Jesus.”

This is the theme of today's sermon: Welcoming the true light and being a disciple of Christ

(1) The Great Light Shining in the Darkness (v.12-16)

This narrative begins with the arrest and imprisonment of John the Baptist.

John, the herald of the Messiah, was captured and imprisoned by Herod Antipas,

who was ruling Galilee.

When Jesus heard the news, he went to Capernaum,

on the northwest coast of the Sea of ​​Galilee

and began preaching in that area.

Please note that the Bible passage states that this is the fulfillment of the prophecy

made by the prophet Isaiah as early as 680 BC,

“ The people walking in darkness have seen a great light; a light has dawned on those living in the land of darkness."(Isaiah 9:1-2)

The people in that land, including Jews and Gentiles,

represents people from all around the world.

Spiritually speaking, they are in the darkness of sin.

The biggest problem in the world is the problem of "sin".

For everyone in the world has sinned and fall short of the glory of God.

They are all in the deep darkness.

No one can find a way out by relying on the the efforts of any man.

The world is living with no hope and no way out.

However, Jesus has now come,

The Messiah and the Prince of Peace prophesied by Isaiah.

He is the "true light" who will disperse the darkness that has shrouded the world,

The one who will bring eternal peace and justice (Isaiah 9:6-7) .

According to God's plan of redemption plan, these Gentiles in Galilee,

sitting in the shadow of death, who originally had no hope of being saved,

will be the first to witness the dawn of the light of salvation,

who will have he opportunity to know Jesus, the light of this world.

Today, we Chinese believers were originally Gentiles, considered as outsiders with regard to the election of Israel.

However, we have received God’s mercy and became members of God’s family.

All this because of the coming of Jesus Christ,

who preached the message of the gospel of the kingdom of heaven

throughout his life (Matthew 4:17),

who suffered, died on the cross, resurrected on the third day, and accomplished salvation, in order to save sinners.

He’s come so that those who believe will not perish but have eternal life.

How precious is this gospel!

However, according to the message as given by Jesus in verse 17,

it first emphasises that it was God's initiative to save—the kingdom of heaven is near!

But then it points out the response that people ought to have: you should repent!

"Repentance" means turning back or turning around.

It is not only to apologize for wrongdoing, but also to confess

that you are a sinner and that you have sinned against God by rebelling against him.

And now you are willing to turn back to God,

An utter change in the way your life was originally heading.

(2) The call to be a disciple (Matthew 4:18-22)

Jesus came to the shore of the Sea of ​​Galilee

(which was actually

an inland lake),

a lake filled with a variety of fish, and an area known for the fishing industry. It’s here that Jesus comes to find and to call His disciples.

"Disciple" means "student" or "follower".

At that time, according to the Jewish culture,

the students would choose their favorite teacher,

and then they would follow him wherever he goes

in order to learn from the teacher's instructions.

However, in this instance, it’s Jesus himself who takes the initiative

to call people to follow him

and shows his sovereignty.

He calls two pairs of fishermen brothers separately, namely,

Simon called Peter (which means Rock) and his brother Andrew,

and the other pair are the sons of Zebedee's, namely, James and John.

Please notice the contents of both the call and their response as recorded in verses 19-20 and 22.

They include the following three characteristics:

A new master in life: "Come and follow me."

Jesus' invitation is a call to be faithful and obedient to Him.

Their response is to decide who is the master of their life.

A new mission and goal:

They’re to move on from their past pursuit of fishing for fish

(that is, material and financial gains),

to to fishing for people,

that is, to work hard for God

and to strive to get more people to convert and follow the Lord.

This is to fulfill the mission of the gospel as the most important goal in life.

New values ​​and priorities:

Simon and Andrew immediately laid down their fishing nets

(that is, their tools for livelihood)

to follow the Lord (verse 20)

James and John left the boat and farewelled their father

to follow Jesus (verse 22).

Their response is to change their value and priority,

They discard the old value, and put the first priority on knowing and following Jesus.

To sum up, vital to what it means to being a disciple is that a disciples is no longer to be self-centered;

Rather, we are to be “Lord Jesus centred”.

We are to put Jesus, the one who loves the people of the world

and sacrificed himself for us,

at the very centre of their lives and as the only master of our lives.

(3) Demonstration and manifestation of the power of the gospel of the kingdom of heaven (23-25)

The missionary work of Jesus in the world shows that the mission of the Kingdom of Heaven includes worship, teaching, preaching, and healing.

It is the gospel of salvation for the whole person.

Only Jesus can give people a completely new life.

The Australian Prime Minister Morrison is a Christian.

After winning the election, he testified that he believed in miracles by declaring, "I believe in miracle"!

The Lord Jesus, the Son of God, came into the world.

His words and his deeds are miracles that saved, healed,

and changed people's lives.

In 4:23 it is recorded that Jesus travelled all over Galilee,

participated in worship in the synagogues,

and taught people according to the Bible

(that is, the Old Testament - the Law, The Prophets and the Psalms).

He preached the gospel of the kingdom of heaven, and healed the people of all kinds of diseases.

This shows that the era of the Messiah’s salvation and reign has begun.

The work of the king of the kingdom of heaven

has brought about the dawn of the kingdom of heaven here on earth.

As well as giving a general introduction to the scope

of the influence of Jesus' ministry,

the author also describes the mode of the gospel ministry that points to the Kingdom of Heaven which includes worship, teaching, preaching, and healing.

For the church today, to have God-centered worship, Bible-based teaching, preaching gospel sermons to unbelievers and caring for the needs of people's body and soul are indispensable tasks.

This call of the gospel of the kingdom of heaven is something

that Jesus himself personally demonstrated.

The disciples followed him in order to observe his example of words and deeds,

to understand and to learn from them,

and then to be sent out by the Lord,

to use what they have learnt and followed,

to become truly effective fishers of men.

To sum up,

Today our world is full of sin and darkness,

With both natural disasters and man-made disasters continuing to occur.

Churches and believers everywhere need to be revived.

First of all, we must return to the starting point of our spiritual life:

We need to have a self-reflection on who is in control of our lives:

Is it sin?

Is it my self-centredness?

Or is the Lord Jesus Christ?

Do I have genuine repentance?

Do I confess that we cannot depend on ourselves to be saved, and that there is no way out?

Jesus, the Messiah, has come.

The true light shines and drives out the darkness.

Our response is to turn and to believe in Him,

We are to take Him as our Lord.

We are to receive the call to be a disciple of Jesus,

To follow Him every day,

and to let the Holy Spirit teach us through the Bible.

We are to learn from the teachings of the Lord Jesus as well as from his mode of service.

We are to obey the Gospel’s Great Commission

And be used by God to become a fishers of men,

In order to glorify the name of the Lord in this world.
