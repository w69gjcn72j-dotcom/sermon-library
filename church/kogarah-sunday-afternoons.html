How a G7 meeting runs

The standing agenda

Ninety minutes. The same shape for all six teams.

  1. Opening prayer
  2. Devotion
  3. KOGCircle — where our people actually are
  4. KOGPathway — First Contact, Face to Face, Meet the Minister, Integration, Church Life, Grow & Serve
  5. The team’s own Term 4 focus
  6. Goals for the next step on the pathway
  7. Actionable steps, and who owns each
  8. Closing prayer

75 households serving densely at St Paul’s Kogarah across three congregations by 31 December 2026.

The lunch

Greet a visitor within two minutes; Meet the Minister within three weeks.

  1. Welcome and grace
  2. Lunch together
  3. Our story — God gathers us at St Paul’s Kogarah to build and to cultivate his house of prayer, which lives and breathes the gospel of Jesus Christ
  4. Their story — how they came, what they are looking for
  5. The next step — a Home Group, Welcome to Life, or a place on a team
  6. Prayer
