Giftedness team

Each member has a gift to use

Our loyalty to one another

Five commitments for every team

Trust grows from small, repeated habits. These are the habits we ask every team to practise.

  1. Cover for one another.Every role should have a trained second person, so that no one carries a ministry alone and no one is left without help when life gets busy.
  2. Speak well of one another.When someone is not in the room, we speak about them as we would want to be spoken about. We praise in public and raise concerns in private.
  3. Go to one another first.If something troubles us, we go to the person directly and gently, before we talk to anyone else about it.
  4. Pray by name.Each team meeting opens with prayer for the people in it, by name, and for the family and work commitments each person carries.
  5. Celebrate faithful service.We thank people for their service, publicly and specifically, and we mark the growth we see, including the first time someone leads something new.

How people join us

Welcome to the Team

Our pathway for those who want to serve is called the Welcome Pathway. It includes the Welcome to Life ministries, the Welcome Home ministries, the Home Group ministries and the Welcome to the Team ministry. Once a person has understood the church's theological principles and practices, they will be provided with the appropriate leaders' training (with safe ministry, if applicable). Each step helps a person know they belong before they are given a task.
